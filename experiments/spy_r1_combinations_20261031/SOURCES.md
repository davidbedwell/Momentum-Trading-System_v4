## Hypotheses introduced here

All Boolean thresholds, SPY persistence definition, volatility stabilization test, R1_proxy gate and conjunction selection in PROTOCOL.md are new research hypotheses. R1_proxy approximates destruction/impulse/breadth persistence/trend repair, omits actual failed rebound measurement and external stress, and does not enforce a fixed trough-relative ordering. It must not be described as the original R-1 implementation. The proxy's same-date conjunction is a simplification of variable-timing morphology and may miss genuine recovery episodes.
