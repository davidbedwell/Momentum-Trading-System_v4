# MTS Manager — Governing Design Contract (2026-10-27)

This is the authoritative design instruction for manager research and implementation. Do not replace it with a simplistic top-N momentum selector. Any deviations require explicit user approval.

1. **Parallel evaluation always.** Evaluate all potentially positive, eligible chromosomes concurrently across available workers. This is computational parallelism, not equal portfolio priority.
2. **Niche first, GA4 fallback.** Determine each stock's controlling niche using only information observable at close t; route native niche-specific GA6/GA7 chromosomes first. If fewer than five DISTINCT actionable STOCKS qualify, expand to GA4; Horizons is the final fallback if needed. The five-stock threshold is not a five-chromosome threshold. Shadow evaluations may cover all eligible families daily in parallel regardless of active selection hierarchy.
3. **All potentially positive opportunities compete.** Evaluate candidate trade probabilities, expected net return, downside/tail loss, expected holding period, transaction costs, and calibration confidence. Multiple chromosomes on one ticker are alternatives, not duplicate positions. Do not equate high win probability with high expected profit.
4. **Rank by expected portfolio contribution.** Select opportunities using probability-weighted expected net profitability, capital employed, holding duration, risk, confidence, and portfolio correlation; do not rank by historical CAGR alone. No fixed requirement to fill ten slots.
5. **Allocate from one shared fictional $100,000 bank.** Initial maximum 10% per ticker during learning; maintain 1–2% equity risk per trade, sector/correlation diversification, next-open fills, realistic cash/fees/settlement, and no universal manager-imposed holding-duration limit. Stronger opportunities get more capital subject to hard risk limits.
6. **Cash is a competing allocation.** Preserve capital for better opportunities that may appear tomorrow. Compare today's marginal expected return with learned future opportunity-arrival value, using only matured historical observations. No peeking at tomorrow's signals. Use a conservative liquidity floor during early training; later learn dynamic reserves from evidence rather than enforcing a permanent arbitrary cash percentage.
7. **Daily portfolio competition.** Reconsider holds, adds, reductions, exits, and replacements every close. A new higher-value opportunity may displace a weaker holding only when incremental expected net benefit exceeds costs and replacement risk. Do not automatically liquidate profitable holdings solely because another signal appears.
8. **Daily exhaustive shadow training.** Every simulated close, independently evaluate eligible chromosomes across families in parallel, log selected and rejected alternatives and counterfactual results; update the learner ONLY after the full outcome horizon has elapsed. Shadow actions never place orders. No future outcome or synthetic generator latent Regime/seed may enter a decision.
9. **Synthetic training before historical certification.** Train on fictional markets, sequential markets with parallel workers inside each market; holdouts remain sealed. Use native GA6/GA7/GA4 chromosome logic and original causally reconstructed features; proxy momentum/reversal is only a smoke test and must not be represented as native training.
10. **Performance objective and evidence.** Maximize compounded portfolio wealth while controlling maximum drawdown at or below 20% target; compare adaptive vs frozen manager on the SAME synthetic scenario using net NAV, CAGR, MDD, Calmar, turnover, capital utilization and replacement outcomes. No success claim from signal-only averages or in-sample results.

Implementation gate: feature-name matching alone is insufficient; verify native feature definitions/scaling, point-in-time inputs, evaluator parity, chronological learning, and shared-capital accounting. Record any incomplete components explicitly. This contract overrides conflicting exploratory defaults such as weekly shadow audits or unconditional top-ten allocation.
