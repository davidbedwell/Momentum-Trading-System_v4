# Frozen design, before setup evaluation

Discovery January 2009–December 2013; annual expanding walk-forward January 2014–November 2018 (December censored). Annual selection uses only prior entries with exit strictly before January entry. Thus December immediately before each evaluation year is embargoed, conservatively even when its exit equals January entry. No OOS tuning. Fixed 64 rules, four families × two choices for each of two core conditions × four optional confirmation sets (none, A, B, both). Rules have 2–4 features. No outcome-fitted numeric thresholds.

Trend/pullback cores: return252>0 OR MA200 slope>0; return21<0 OR MA20 distance<0. Confirmations return5>0 and positive_fraction21>0.5.
Stabilization cores: return21<0 OR MA20 distance<0; acceleration21>0 OR reversal5_vs_prior21>0. Confirmations return5>0 and vol10<vol21.
Volume normalization cores: inferred prior15/60 mean volume>1.2 OR >1.5; volume5/20<1 OR <0.8. Confirmations return21<0 and return5>0. Prior15/60=(20*volume20/60-5*volume5/60)/15. This is elevated prior-period volume followed by normalization, not proof of an event-day spike.
Relative strength cores: return21<0 OR MA20 distance<0; RS21>0 OR RS21/21>prior42_RS/42. Confirmations return5>0 and return252>0. Prior42 RS reconstructed from 63- and 21-session stock and universe compounded returns.

For each family and all families: eligible training rules need >=40 selected labeled observations and >=24 active months. Rank by unweighted average monthly (selected winner fraction minus entire basket winner fraction), on active months; tie-break pooled enrichment, then rule ID. Pick highest even if negative; export failures too. Freeze 2013 shortlist and annually reselect expanding with same criterion. Compare single-family and overall policies, fixed rules, baseline, and complements. No OOS-based winner declaration. Export discovery-ranked tables with OOS fields for diagnostics; never rank by OOS.

Selection is conjunction of strict conditions; unknown inputs fail selection and are counted separately. No minimum holdings or outcome-aware replacement. Empty selection earns cash zero. All portfolios equal-weight at common monthly entry opens and hold to common next entry opens, with drift-adjusted traded notional ×10bps per side, including initial purchase and final liquidation. Missing realized returns keep their preassigned weight in zero-return cash proxy, explicitly flagged; classification excludes missing labels. Monthly MDD and monthly Sharpe only. Primary benchmark entire supplied RSI basket; same fixed 77-ticker source universe. All strategy comparisons on identical 59 OOS months. No 2019+ raw data needed or loaded. Earnings unavailable absent verifiable dated PIT coverage. No p-values or significance claims.
