# Multihorizon follow-up
Fixed before computing outcomes. Read only the original selection/inventory and underlying archived ledgers; never import or execute the original study. Preserve identity sample and retrospective winners as separate overlapping analyses. Missing ledgers are missing observations, never zero trades. GA8 newly available selected annual production models are analyzed separately with net return in place of risk-unit r; no identity sample is claimed. Open positions excluded by the source reconstruction remain excluded.

Compute DEV79 cross-sectional fraction strictly above trailing simple MA10/20/50/100/200 with complete windows and at least 20 eligible stocks. Reindex to SPY sessions before rolling; no fill. Entry uses prior session close. For each breadth compute changes D_h=B_t-B_(t-h), velocity D_h/h and acceleration (B_t-2B_(t-h)+B_(t-2h))/h² for h=5,10,21. Velocity is an exact rescaling of change and gets no duplicate hypothesis test. No thresholds fitted. A common complete-feature sample compares all horizons fairly.

Outcomes: ledger r (risk units, not percentage price return) and pnl>0. MAE only if recorded; do not invent it from final P&L. Single-feature OLS residualized within chromosome × entry year, separately by GA system and analysis set. Effects per pooled within-cell SD. Calendar-month clustered t inference keeps cross-sectional chromosome copies together; overlapping trades may still span clusters. BH and Bonferroni across all pooled feature/outcome/system/set tests, including winner diagnostics. Annual, family, chromosome and leave-one-year-out slopes assess heterogeneity; these are descriptive, not additional discovery tests. Require >=30 trades, >=12 entry months for inference. Selection is retrospective and results exploratory, not causal or untouched out-of-sample. Fixed retrospective universe/current adjusted prices prevent certified point-in-time universe/vintage claims.
