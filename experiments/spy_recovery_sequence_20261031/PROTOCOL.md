# Ordered recovery hypothesis — frozen before outcomes
2026-10-31. Exploratory fixed-rule study; prior human inspection includes 2017–2025. No untouched OOS, original R1 parity, causal treatment effect, or executable trading claim. No tuning, GA, paid data, network acquisition, MTS changes or certification.

Primary sample 2006–2025; 2005 warmup, 2026 excluded. Annual chronological summaries purge forward labels at every year boundary, as does pooled analysis. No rule selection or parameter fitting. All windows count SPY trading sessions.

New hypothesis, not an original R1 implementation: damage gate = SPY adjusted close / trailing 252-session high -1 <= -10% AND DEV79 above SMA50 fraction <40%. Breadth recovery = breadth at least 10 percentage points above its value at damage declaration AND trailing five-session breadth mean >=40%. Price confirmation = SPY above EMA20 on >=4/5 sessions, positive trailing five-session return, EMA20 rising over five sessions AND RV20 <= RV20 five sessions ago. All inputs through close t only, full rolling observations, >=20 eligible stocks, no filling. No troughs, external stress, or actual failed-rebound measurement.

FSM: IDLE -> DAMAGE -> BREADTH -> CONFIRMED. Damage starts at first eligible gated session when armed; only one transition per session, strictly later timestamps. DAMAGE waits at most 63 sessions for breadth; BREADTH waits at most 21 sessions for price. Transition allowed at exact deadline; timeout on following session. CONFIRMED persists for 21 sessions including declaration. Episodes hard-expire after 126 sessions. Missing any input aborts episode and yields missing state, not false. After termination, a new episode requires the damage gate to have been false on an idle eligible session; no overlapping episodes or resetting at future lows. Initial state armed. State occupancy is primary; transition pulses secondary. No conditioning earlier-stage observations on eventual successful conversion. Breadth anchor is declaration breadth, never subsequent minimum.

Compare each stage with preceding stage (damage with unconditional); all with weak breadth alone, SPY positive 63-session momentum, unconditional and contemporaneous matched damage controls. Controls are DAMAGE-stage dates, exactly stratified by calendar year, current trailing drawdown bins [-inf,-20,-10,-5,0,inf] percent and annualized RV20 bins [0,20,35,inf] percent. Weight controls to target-date stratum distribution; unmatched targets excluded only from matched contrast. No matching on future outcome or eventual conversion. Match support/counts exported. Same-date conditioning controls current severity imperfectly; it cannot isolate ordering causally. Export same-date unordered conjunction as an additional descriptive comparison.

Labels: forward 21/63-session adjusted-close return, probability return <0, maximum peak-to-trough close drawdown over t..t+h. Positive MDD effect means less loss. Marginal calendar HAC influence CIs lag max(63,2h); weighted paired scores for matched estimates. Minimum five dates per arm for inference, fewer than 20 runs or greedily spaced h-session windows flagged fragile; counts are not independence. BH and Bonferroni across ALL finite pooled contrasts (including pulses), separately annual, separately full sensitivity family. BH dependent-test guarantees limited; rare-state normal CIs can be unreliable. Additional paired circular block bootstrap 63/126 sessions, 1000 replicates, fixed seed, for occupancy breadth/confirmed versus prior/weak/unconditional, all horizons/metrics; matching not bootstrapped. Marginal intervals, not selection-adjusted.

Sensitivity: wait windows (42,10), (84,42), confirmed retention 10/42, one-day delay of ALL signals and matching covariates, HAC lag126, damage threshold -15%, omit each crisis year 2008/2009/2020/2022 and all jointly, omit each individual sample year. No sensitivity winner. Annual results 2006–2025 are expanding-calendar historical diagnostics, not untouched rolling validation. Exclusions remove both signal dates and labels whose forward paths touch omitted years.

A robust incremental claim requires positive return uplift versus both prior stage and weak breadth, with Bonferroni p<.05, adequate counts, direction persisting under crisis exclusion and delay at BOTH horizons. Drawdown improvement assessed separately; otherwise report no established incremental benefit. Descriptive means and all nulls retained.
