# Execution specification (exploratory; not a prospective preregistration)

Question: does prior-close DEV79 breadth identify profitable entries in archived GA6/GA7/GA8 chromosomes? Analyze dated saved trades only. Keep native GA6, common-engine GA6/GA7 and GA8 fold results separate. Selection, eligibility and statistical limits are in REPORT.md.

Thresholds: weak <.4; medium [.4,.6]; strong >.6; >=20 stocks with trailing 50-session SMA. Features use prior completed session, never future trough/crossing. Improvement: breadth difference over five sessions >0. Crossing: last observed up-crossing of .4; compare ages 0–5 against weak, describe ages 6–21. SPY outcomes: 5/21/63 sessions from entry close. No future profitable spell or winner labels enter predictors.

Bounded common-engine cohort: all 400 saved GA6 candidates; first 20 lexically sorted GA7 IDs in each of 14 groups; separately saved GA7 top 140 and common-CAGR GA6 top ten. The deterministic sample avoids selection on this study's outcomes but does not undo original survivor selection and is not guaranteed representative. No new evolutionary search or source-engine replay.

Outcomes: preserve common R/P&L; GA8 whole position episodes reconstructed from fills with adds/reductions and dividend entitlement, execution slippage already in prices and 1bp commission. Native GA6 uses saved return/MAE with unverified accounting provenance. Do not pool these units.

Controls: within chromosome; exact matched ticker, year, momentum sign, volatility bands and observed holding bin. Report lost support. Holding matching is post-treatment descriptive. Calendar-aligned HAC63 and HAC126 account for shared shocks; show BH per family and all reported contrasts. Annual and retrospective-top comparisons remain exploratory. Report close-path risk proxies separately from true execution MAE and NAV MDD. No external data, deployment or certification.
