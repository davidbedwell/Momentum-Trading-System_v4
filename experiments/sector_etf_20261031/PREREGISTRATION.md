# Independent sector ETF experiment: frozen protocol

Registered before downloading or calculating price outcomes. UTC registration time and SHA256 are recorded in preregistration.lock.json. Hard ceiling: four hours from registration; no paid data, external publication, main MTS or prior Stage2 edits. Only this directory may be changed. No stock signals or original MTS H3/H4/H6 are used; the H labels below belong solely to this experiment.

## Data and sample
Free Yahoo daily chart adjusted close is the first source; an existing genuinely free raw-price cache or free alternative is permitted only with documented provenance and dividend/split adjustment. Stop with blockers if adjusted data cannot be obtained. No synthetic history or index substitution. Download 1993-01-01 through 2025-12-31, using earlier observations only for warmup. Exclude 2026 entirely. Universe: XLB XLE XLF XLI XLK XLP XLU XLV XLY (inception 1998-12-16), XLRE (2015-10-07), XLC (2018-06-18); SPY benchmark (1993-01-22). SGOV defensive asset (2020-05-26), only after actual first valid price. Inception is an eligibility lower bound, not an invented first trading date. No pre-inception fills. Require full observed lookback and current price; require at least 9 eligible sectors. Structural changes to sector classifications and current-family selection are limitations.

Discovery: return dates 2000-01-01 through 2016-12-31. Sealed validation: 2017-01-01 through 2025-12-31. No strategy/parameter selection from validation. Diagnostic discovery halves: 2000-2008, 2009-2016; validation halves: 2017-2020, 2021-2025. No outcomes examined before this file. Compute and persist discovery first; seal code and discovery hashes before explicit validation run. Validation is one historical holdout, not an independently custodial blind sample. Remove forward observations crossing split or subperiod boundaries (including overlapping 3-month labels).

## Hypotheses and fixed implementations
H1: positive cross-sectional predictive association of trailing six calendar month sector total return relative to SPY with subsequent 1 and 3 month relative return. H2: same with twelve months. No skip month. H3: negative prior one month relative return (reversal score) predicts positive subsequent one month relative return. Relative return = sector growth divided by SPY growth minus one. Signals observed at last session close of each month. Equal-weight top 3 and bottom 3 with deterministic ticker tie-break. Monthly rebalance, long-only top3 investable portfolio; top-minus-bottom spread is a diagnostic portfolio, gross 200% and net 0%, with explicit two-sided trading costs, excludes borrow/financing and is not a deployable short claim. Three-month horizons are prediction diagnostics, not separately optimized trading strategies. Include equal-weight available sectors as a baseline.

H4: SPY adjusted close above trailing 200-session arithmetic moving average (including signal day) predicts useful exposure risk control. Standalone daily all-SPY else defensive holding, no sector combinations. Compare with SPY buy-and-hold and defensive buy-and-hold. Defensive = zero-interest USD cash before SGOV is actually available, then SGOV total return after one observed valid SGOV day; also run H4 with zero-interest cash throughout to show dependence on the cash proxy. No SGOV backfill, no invented Treasury yields. This hybrid is conservative historically and not a historical risk-free rate series.

Execution: use vendor-provided adjusted open only if directly available. Otherwise use **one-session delayed close-to-close**: month-end signal executes next trading session close; daily trend signal executes next session close. Raw opens adjusted by a contemporaneous close factor are not treated as vendor adjusted opens. Report timing clearly. Actual holdings drift with prices between rebalances. Costs 0.0015 and 0.003 per dollar bought/sold, including SGOV; cash incurs no transaction cost. Apply entry and final liquidation cost in each reported period; SPY buy hold also pays entry/exit. No leverage for long-only portfolios, no tax, market impact or short borrow model. Total-return adjusted close reinvestment is a vendor approximation.

## Measurement and inference
For every month compute cross-sectional Spearman rank IC and top3-minus-bottom3 forward total-return spread (1/3m as applicable); do not treat 9-11 ETF observations as independent samples. Inference uses time series of monthly IC/spreads: Newey-West mean SE with fixed 6 monthly lags, two-sided normal p-values; circular moving-block bootstrap 12-month blocks, 2000 replications, seed 20261031, percentile 95% intervals. Preserve contemporaneous sector dependence by aggregating by month first. For 3m overlapping labels use same 6-lag HAC and block bootstrap. Holm correction across the five H1/H2/H3 IC endpoints per split; spread p-values descriptive. Report count, mean IC, confidence intervals, p-values, spread and fixed top3 portfolio. Small cross sections and common macro exposure limit inference.

Report daily net CAGR (calendar elapsed time), maximum drawdown including initial wealth=1, Calmar, annualized volatility, turnover, and monthly net active return mean with HAC and bootstrap for top3 versus SPY. Report each chronological split/subsplit and full period (full period descriptive only). H4 paired monthly differences versus SPY: block bootstrap for difference in CAGR and absolute MDD improvement; H4 tests at 15bps use Holm correction across two paired endpoint p-values derived from bootstrap sign tails (descriptive approximate inference). 30bps is fixed sensitivity.

## Frozen decision rule
No production/deployment GO from this bounded study. GO for a new independently preregistered replication only if a sector hypothesis has validation Holm-adjusted IC p<0.05 and positive IC in both discovery and validation for its tested horizon, plus positive validation 15bps spread and, for its fixed monthly top3 implementation, positive validation CAGR excess over SPY at both costs. H4 merits replication only if validation absolute MDD improves at least 20%, Calmar exceeds SPY at both costs, and both paired validation bootstrap intervals exclude zero in the desired direction (CAGR difference positive and MDD improvement positive). Otherwise STOP/inconclusive; do not retune. Distinguish economic failure from imprecise evidence. Report all endpoints, including unfavorable ones.
