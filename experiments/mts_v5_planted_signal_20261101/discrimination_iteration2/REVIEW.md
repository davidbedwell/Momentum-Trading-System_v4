# Review and interpretation

Reviewed five_tier_discrimination and opportunity_evaluator_fix experiment.py, reports, preregistrations, tests and finalizers; scaling_robustness failure summary; original /opt/Momentum-Trading-System_v5-alpha/discovery/competitive_ga/functional_niche_manager.py; repaired snapshot causal_ledger.py. Paths and hashes are in source_checksums.json.

The original manager validates dated proposals, comparable EV units and funded execution. Quality is supplied by the conditional_ev callback: its contract cannot certify provenance or recognize five opportunity classes. The causal ledger verifies decision/outcome timestamps and immutability, but has no conditional return estimator. Its available() permits maturity <= cutoff, while this experiment conservatively requires maturity < cutoff.

The prior five-tier frozen estimator failed at inversion (989 false GREAT /2394 true TERRIBLE), while rolling repair reduced this to22 but removed nearly all classification. Neither result established middle-class identification. Previous null scaling experiments also showed confidently miscalibrated signals could increase tails: therefore no scaling expansion is attempted here.

The economic bands are disjoint. infer() supports every band and requires the full interval in one band. A middle band has width0.004 and half-width0.002. An80%-faithful coarse sensor plus20% independent random classes implies E[net|cell] approximately0.8*class_mean in the balanced stable DGP. Thus POOR/GOOD cells center near -0.0032/+0.0032, only0.0012 away from one boundary. A diagnostic radius above0.0012 alone makes those cells UNKNOWN. Date-common noise0.003 and idiosyncratic noise0.008, mixed class membership and at most21 recent cell-date clusters make such radii unsurprising. Counting all stocks as independent would incorrectly tighten uncertainty. Exact saved-record diagnostics in report.json distinguish middle-centered UNKNOWN, radius<0.002, intervals fitting a middle band despite other gates, and reasons; they overlap and are descriptive, not causal estimates.

Enhancement adds two Gaussian-noisy latent measurements, sd0.30/0.50 class units. Independent errors and known relative sensor variances are assumptions of the injected experiment. They are observable before execution by construction; neither is a label or future return. These assumptions have no demonstrated real-market realism. Null removes association and inversion flips the measurement relationship without an observable warning. Realized tape and costs are shared across the three policies. Comparison changes information and estimation jointly; there is no estimator-only causal claim.

Primary estimator uses six pseudo-date hierarchical shrinkage toward a learned linear parent and an isotonic fit oriented using matured data; inversion may learn descending order. Diagnostic intervals add sampling uncertainty, recent-window discrepancy and shrinkage displacement. Issued-forecast residuals are date clustered and available only after maturity. Drift compares old/recent cell-date means, quarantines all decisions and resets the history floor. UNKNOWN is retained for insufficient dates, absent identifiable spread, boundary uncertainty, quarantine and residual bias.

Protocol limitation: the primary recent window is pooled into the estimated mean. Its discrepancy is a stability diagnostic, not independent calibration. This was explicitly specified in the frozen method, but falls short of the requested independent-calibration standard. The reserved static ablation fits the estimate only on signals0..77, evaluates calibration80..121 and scores126..249 without refitting; it cannot replace causal rolling evaluation or certify the complete online algorithm. All result interpretation must retain this limitation; parameters were not repaired using holdout outcomes.

A stronger sensor can be confidently wrong immediately after an unannounced inversion. Detector latency and false GREAT must be checked against the repaired comparator on the same new tapes. Increased stable coverage or profitable portfolios cannot override this safety comparison. Diagnostic SE intervals are neither formal prediction intervals for latent classes nor distribution-free coverage guarantees. The experiment reports failure whenever the preregistered all-seed criteria fail.
