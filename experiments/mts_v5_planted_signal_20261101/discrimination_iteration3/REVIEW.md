# Review and inference limits

Reviewed iteration2 README, preregistration, full report (including static ablation, follow-up diagnosis, per-seed scores and ledgers), experiment/test/finalizer scripts, opportunity_evaluator_fix inference/callback/replay implementation, original native functional_niche_manager, and the repaired causal ledger. Imported dependencies are hashed in source_checksums.json. The local manager/kernel/patch snapshots preserve the inherited execution seam; production sources are unchanged.

The native manager requires causal, comparable cost-adjusted conditional_ev from its caller. It cannot establish measurement provenance or a five-class quality model. The prior causal ledger enforces immutable timestamped outcomes and availability; availability alone does not establish independent calibration or predictive information.

Iteration2 primary estimates pooled recent calibration observations into their point estimates. Its separate static ablation had independent splits but retained an uncertainty formula inherited from the online model and lacked portfolio replay or drift protection. Sparse per-cell dates, random class mixing, date-common noise, short windows and repeated nuisance alarms explain middle abstention without any tier-dispatch bug.

Iteration3 keeps all sensor inputs, seeds, costs and tapes unchanged. Training uses date means clipped at +/-5% (bounded influence against extreme return contamination), aggregated into fixed five-date blocks. Two pseudo blocks pool toward adjacent-cell training means; the entire shrinkage displacement remains an uncertainty penalty. Calibration uses only independent chronological residual blocks to enlarge intervals, not alter point estimates. No stock-level independence is assumed. This deliberately modest pooling cannot remove calibration sample uncertainty or learn absent information.

There are only 16 effective training blocks and eight calibration blocks. Normal approximations and the fixed correlation block length are assumptions, not finite-sample guarantees. The interval targets a stationary conditional mean for a measurement cell, not a future individual return. The ordinal posterior integrates that approximate conditional-mean distribution over the fixed economic bands. It is not a calibrated probability of a hidden individual generative class: that class is evaluator-only, and cannot be calibrated against oracle labels. Confusion against hidden EV tiers and noisy realized tiers are distinct outputs.

The holdout monitor is safety-only and consumes strictly matured high-minus-low outcome contrasts on fixed nonoverlapping five-date blocks. It cancels additive date-common shocks, reduces multiplicity to 26 prespecified looks, and latches abstention. Its Gaussian union-bound threshold includes baseline uncertainty; estimated variance, serial dependence and heavy tails prevent a distribution-free sequential false-alarm claim. No post-alarm relearning is permitted, so loss of coverage under persistent drift is intentional. An inversion with no advance observable warning is causally indistinguishable before outcomes mature.

The four synthetic tapes were already evaluated in iteration2. Iteration3 holds outcomes out from fitting/calibration but does not create a fresh confirmatory test set. No tuning after any iteration3 holdout scores. No empirical real-market PIT signals, release/revision histories, sensor covariance or independent regime episodes are supplied. Stable synthetic association is planted by construction; a failure here cannot prove real-world absence of alpha, and a pass would not establish real-market edge.
