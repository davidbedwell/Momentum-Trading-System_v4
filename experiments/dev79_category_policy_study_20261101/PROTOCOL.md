# Frozen Phase 1 protocol
Written before execution of this study. No selector will be fitted or tested in this run.

Universe, four policies and execution are unchanged from the four-policy experiment. Copied executable snapshot; no production imports/writes. Claude, Gemini and Grok DEV79 are approximations; exact Claude/Grok source unavailable. One CPU, 20 minute ceiling. No search or threshold fitting.

Decision at close t has category computed strictly through previous global session t-1, even though policies themselves may use close t. All indicators use complete unfilled rolling windows. Market benchmark is local SPY close (not dividend-adjusted close); no verified historical sector mapping/sector return archive was established. SPY relative strength is stock 20-session close return minus SPY 20-session close return. Missing SPY/history/OHLCV makes state unknown. Present-day revisions/adjustments remain a PIT limitation.

Five predefined states, ordered priority, first match wins. At t-1: r5=C/C[-5]-1; r20=C/C[-20]-1; old5=C[-5]/C[-10]-1; ma20 and ma50 are trailing means; rs20=r20-SPY r20; vr=V/mean20(V). Require 51 consecutive valid positive OHLC, nonnegative volume, and valid market r20. Priority:
1. reversal: C>ma20, r5>0, C[-5]<=ma20[-5], and C[-20]/C[-40]-1<0.
2. deterioration: C<ma50 and (r20<0 or rs20<0).
3. acceleration: C>ma20>ma50, r5>old5, r20>0, rs20>0, vr>=1.
4. uptrend: C>ma50, r20>0, rs20>=0.
5. consolidation: all remaining complete-history cases. This is an exhaustive residual state, not a claim of narrow trading range.
Unknown is separate and never reassigned using outcomes.

Discovery: 2009-01-02 through 2017-12-29. Later test: 2018-01-02 through 2026-09-14. Require outcome end within each segment; embargo the boundary-crossing horizon. These are chronological descriptive splits, not pristine external OOS: underlying policies/archive were previously examined. No tuning on either segment. No retrospective switching.

Common opportunities: every decision date/ticker, including hold and abstain, for all four policies. Primary matched set requires all four ledgers, valid execution open t+1 and horizon-end open t+2, positive closes t and t+1 (so policy portfolio NAV has contemporaneous marks), and no segment crossing. Terminal and missing outcomes excluded and counted. Unavailable listings excluded, unknown history retained separately. One-session horizon uses raw split-adjusted opens without dividends. Identical same-ticker hold benchmark holds pre-execution shares unchanged and incurs no transaction cost. A flat stock inventory has a cash benchmark of zero. Also report all-opportunity stock buy-and-hold forward return independent of policy.

Use actual clipped fills under original cash, admission caps and T+1 settlement; q0 = decision-close shares, q1 = q0 + signed filled shares. Local incremental dollars versus hold = (q1-q0)*(O[t+2]-O[t+1]) - execution fee - adverse slippage dollars at t+1. Divide by common per-policy execution-open NAV to get local NAV contribution. Open NAV is previous close NAV + held-share close-to-open move. This is a controlled one-session mark-to-market attribution of each historical action, not a standalone alternate portfolio or realized multi-session causal treatment effect. Subsequent decisions/costs at t+2 are outside this horizon. It does not assign the overnight t-to-t+1 gap to a decision that had not executed. Fees and realized sell PnL are also attributed separately to the originating pre-decision category, on all actual fills; realized PnL includes past inventory and is not category alpha. Exposure/turnover/selection differences persist. Report q0 and q1 exposure and action counts to expose them.

Primary pair contrasts use exactly matched date/ticker opportunities, including zeros. Aggregate incremental NAV contributions over tickers within category/day; compare policies on same calendar days, with category-absent days zero. This measures actual-sized contribution rather than a category-specific NAV. Also report narrower all-four-flat and all-four-held support; these are diagnostics and not randomized action assignment. Date/ticker matching cannot equalize inventory, sizing, prior history, cash constraints or selection. Never name a category winner from conditional NAV.

Uncertainty: resample calendar-month blocks of paired daily contrasts, 2,000 deterministic bootstrap draws (seed 79), preserving contemporaneous cross-stock dependence; report 95% intervals plus Bonferroni 99.8333% intervals across 5 categories x 6 pairs within each segment. Monthly blocks are an approximation, not full protection against long memory. Minimum support: 100 discordant filled-action opportunities and 24 months containing such opportunities per category/pair/segment; otherwise inconclusive. Passing support and statistical intervals still do not establish a selector because policy-path confounding remains. Phase 1 stops with measured results.
