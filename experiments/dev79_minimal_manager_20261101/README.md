# Minimal historical DEV79 daily manager

Implemented isolated research replay. Start with `RESULTS.md` for actual results and commands. Run the complete pipeline using `bash experiments/dev79_minimal_manager_20261101/run.sh` from the repository root. Uses the existing `/opt/mts/.venv`; dependencies and versions are recorded in `requirements-observed.txt`. No downloads or production modifications.

`manager.py` computes all features from archived OHLCV only: close/100-session SMA trend, 20/63-session momentum, annualized 20-session volatility, 63-session momentum minus contemporaneous cross-sectional median, 20-session high-low range, and current volume / 20-session mean volume. Its score combines cross-sectional percentile ranks: 0.3 trend + 0.3 momentum63 + 0.2 relative strength − 0.2 volatility. Relative strength and momentum ranks are intentionally redundant in this simple rule, not independent evidence. Rolling windows require consecutive observed sessions; prices and features are never backfilled. Pre-2009 data supply causal warmup.

Daily eligibility requires positive trend and both momentums, volatility below 60%, relative volume above 0.25 and mean daily range below 10%. Select up to ten highest scores, ties by ticker. Target weight is min(10%, 2%/annualized volatility), floored volatility 10%. Scale total targets to 90% gross, 45% when fewer than half the observed stocks have positive trend, or at most 30% below a 15% portfolio drawdown. Rebalance when target/current dollar difference exceeds max($25, 0.5% NAV); fully exit deselected positions. This supports enter, hold, add, reduce, exit and abstain without learned models. These parameters were fixed before the corrected replay; no optimization occurred.

Orders are created after close with that close's dollar targets, executed at the next global session's actual open. Sell first; proceeds become available next session. Buys are clipped by settled cash and, for the active strategies, 10% symbol and 90% aggregate open-value admission limits. Existing holdings can drift above limits after gaps; limits are not guaranteed losses or intraday controls. All arms pay 5 bps fee and 5 bps adverse slippage each side. No same-day sale funding, leverage, shorts, intraday stops, cash yield, tax or fitted impact model. Fractional shares are permitted. The final NAV is marked to close, without terminal liquidation; end-of-sample orders remain explicitly unexecuted.

Buy-and-hold reserves initial capital/79 per ticker, buying after its first observed close in the replay period and holding indefinitely. The last purchase may be slightly cash-clipped by cumulative costs. Capital for future listings sits in cash, which is explicitly a retrospective fixed-universe convention. Momentum rebalances every 21 sessions into up to ten positive 63-session momentum names at 9% targets. Same dates, price basis, next-open timing, fees, slippage and settlement scenario apply throughout; portfolios have different exposure rules by design.

## Source and limitations

The source is `/opt/mts/portfolio_evolution_dev80/dev80_raw_no_smci.parquet`, traced through `/opt/mts/ga5_redesign/launch_exploratory_79.py`. It contains 394,795 actual historical bars and 79 tickers through September 14, 2026. `audit_inputs.py` checks exact OHLC, corporate-action and security identity agreement with the historical enriched panel in `/opt/mts/ga6/data/ga6d_defensive_features.parquet`. The enriched panel's `volume` is a signed transformed feature; it is deliberately excluded. The raw archive has actual nonnegative share volume. The volume gate is dimensionless; no absolute adjusted-price or dollar-volume cutoff is used, avoiding dependence on future split adjustment scale. Source SHA256s and per-ticker coverage are recorded.

Prices are split-adjusted: the existing 2009–2018 split audit and a new full-source continuity check support this treatment (all 35 splits of factor >=1.5 favor adjusted continuity). Do not multiply holdings by split factors again. This empirical continuity check is not independent vendor certification. Dividends are archived but excluded from ALL arms because payment dates and adjusted per-share cash basis are unverified. Results are **price-return only**, not total returns. Missing next-open prices cancel orders; no fill price is invented. Missing held closing marks use the last observed close for valuation only and are logged separately (none occurred in this replay).

DEV79 is a retrospective survivor universe, not a historical index roster. Membership, vendor as-of timestamps, static historical sector classifications, earnings availability and corporate-action cash timing lack PIT certification in the existing provenance gate. This implementation does not use sector, earnings, GA, niche, precomputed predictive features or synthetic planted signals. Historical adjustment restatements and source collection bias remain. The tests establish code-level timing and accounting consistency; they cannot certify historical source availability. No alpha, OOS validation, transferability or certification claim is made. No model review, monitoring or production changes.

## Reuse, tests and artifacts

`trade_accounting.py` is copied unchanged from `experiments/mts_v5_manager_audit_20261031/reference_snapshot/ga7/trade_accounting.py`. It handles weighted cost basis for adds/partial reductions and realized PnL. The close/next-open pending-order structure, next-session sale settlement and independent NAV/PnL reconciliation follow the accounting patterns inspected in `experiments/synthetic_cash_e2e_20261101/run.py` and the repaired replay kernel, without importing any synthetic generator, learned predictor or unrelated runtime dependencies.

Executable tests cover future mutation and prefix invariance, real next-session opens, missing-open cancellation, partial sales, costs, settlement, cash clipping, missing-feature abstention, all six manager actions, position count, and independent daily reconstruction of all three historical ledgers. `input_audit.json` and `split_continuity.csv` hold input checks; `provenance.json` records source hashes and limitations and copies the existing provenance/split audits. `features.csv.gz` audits every date/ticker feature. Each strategy directory contains decisions, orders (including canceled and terminal pending orders), fills, positions, daily cash/unsettled/NAV/PnL, and stale-mark logs. `comparison.csv`, `metrics.json`, logs, runtime, and SHA256 `checksums.json` make the run reviewable. Fill count is not round-trip trade count; turnover is summed traded open notional divided by initial capital. CAGR uses 252 sessions/year; Sharpe uses zero risk-free return.
