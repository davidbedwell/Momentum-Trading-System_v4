# Frozen design before validation computation
Date: 2026-10-31. Independent descriptive hypothesis study. No GA, fitting of thresholds, paid data, MTS changes, or pushes.

Hypotheses operationalize the user's summary of prior Claude/Grok ideas; original transcripts were not supplied. Fixed rules: 21/63-session momentum, 21-session momentum acceleration, 20/60-session realized volatility ratio >1.2 or <0.8, breadth above 50/200-session simple averages >60% or <40%, 21-session breadth change >10 or <-10 percentage points, positive SPY momentum with falling breadth (divergence), negative medium-term momentum with improving short momentum and breadth (recovery), and trend/volatility/breadth combinations. DEV panels evaluated separately when accessible, never pooled as independent replication.

Discovery signal dates 2006-2016; each forward outcome must also end by 2016-12-31. Validation signal dates 2017-2025; outcomes must end by 2025-12-31. 2005 warmup only; 2026 excluded. No validation threshold changes. Primary discovery selection: largest absolute 21-session return uplift vs unconditional baseline, with >=100 signals and >=20 signal onsets; choose one rule and freeze its observed discovery direction. All validation rules reported regardless of result. Primary validation claim: selected rule's 21-session return uplift in discovery direction, two-sided 95% HAC interval excluding zero and validation BH q<0.05; other endpoints exploratory. No selection if no eligible rule.

Targets: close t to close t+h adjusted return; negative return probability; maximum peak-to-trough drawdown of adjusted closes including t..t+h (negative). Secondary minimum return relative to t also saved. These are predictive labels, not executable close-t fills. Secondary long/cash strategy delays signal by one full session, executing at t+1 close and earning t+1 to t+2; costs 5/10/25 bps per one-way exposure change; zero cash yield. No optimization of costs or strategy.

Effect sizes vs same-period unconditional and positive 63-session momentum baseline; signal and baseline must both have observed rule inputs. HAC/Newey-West influence-function CIs for conditional means and paired conditional-minus-baseline effects, Bartlett lag max(63,2*h); normal approximation. Overlapping labels and persistent states mean daily counts are not independent; report onsets and greedy nonoverlap counts. BH across all rule x horizon x three outcome tests separately per split and comparator (including simple momentum); selected validation remains subject to this broad family. CIs marginal, not simultaneous. Regime/year summaries descriptive, not used for selection. Sensitivity: 126-lag CIs for selected rule.

DEV universes are retrospective and survivorship-biased. Adjusted price histories are current-vintage, not point-in-time data. Breadth denominator includes only stocks with current and complete trailing prices; no backfill/forward fill. At least 20 eligible stocks. Missing outcomes/inputs excluded, never interpreted as false. DEV87 inaccessible if source and local copy fail; explicitly report limitation and execute available data.
