# MTS manager — conditional path EV and position management design (2026-10-27)

User-proposed design additions, to incorporate into research and implementation. Read alongside MANAGER_GOVERNING_CONTRACT.md and MANAGER_HOLD_DECISIONS.md.

1. CONDITIONAL REMAINING-PATH EV. At each decision close, learn the conditional distribution of outcomes from the current observable state, not the original entry state: probabilities of next target versus stop/trailing stop, expected R-multiple under HOLD/ENTER/INCREASE/REDUCE/EXIT/CASH, remaining net EV, and full MAE/MFE path statistics. Penalize adverse excursion and fragile tail paths, but avoid double-counting downside penalties already present in the objective.
2. CURRENT R AND PATH QUALITY. Measure open R captured, realized R, MAE/MFE ratio, path smoothness and whipsaw, time in trade, and conditional edge decay. Duration is a feature and chromosome recommendation, NOT a universal manager holding-period cap.
3. OPPORTUNITY COST AND CAPITAL EFFICIENCY. Compare expected remaining marginal net contribution of incumbent capital with best actionable alternatives, including cash/risk-free return, costs, settlement delay, uncertainty and capital employed over comparable forward horizons.
4. REGIME AND VOLATILITY. Feed causally observable regime/volatility context or continuous features into chromosomes so evolution can learn thresholds/subrules that differ by market environment. Never use hindsight regime labels or generator latent states.
5. RISK-CONSISTENT SCALING. Increase only when incremental after-cost EV at increased size is positive and total portfolio risk remains within hard limits. Reduce or exit if marginal EV is negative or portfolio risk approaches limits, subject to competing actions and mandatory defense/risk rules. Fixed-fractional/volatility-based portfolio risk governor is external; chromosome recommends timing and magnitude within envelope.

ARCHITECTURE: Chromosomes provide conditional outcome distributions and action recommendations; manager performs daily portfolio-wide competition among HOLD/ENTER/INCREASE/REDUCE/EXIT/CASH using shared capital, risk, correlation and settlement. Learning uses only matured, point-in-time outcomes; train/validation/holdout remain separate. Do not claim validated calibration or trading performance until chronological native replay and parity tests are completed.
