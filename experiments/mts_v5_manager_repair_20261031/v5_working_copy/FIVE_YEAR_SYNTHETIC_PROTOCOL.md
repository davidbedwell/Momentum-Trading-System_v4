# Five-year chronological synthetic development protocol

Use eligible GA6 and GA7 chromosomes, controlling-niche-first routing, identical after-cost risk gates, and explicit NO_TRADE. GA4/Horizons are research-only unless separately approved with validated exits.

1. Fit manager using Year 1 only. Freeze manager and evaluate Year 2 once.
2. Inspect Year 2 failures, refit on Years 1–2, freeze and evaluate Year 3 once.
3. Inspect Year 3 failures, refit on Years 1–3, freeze and evaluate Year 4 once.
4. Inspect Year 4 failures, refit on Years 1–4, freeze and evaluate Year 5 once.
5. Inspect Year 5, learn from Years 1–5, then move to a new untouched five-year synthetic set. Never call refitted results on previously seen years independent certification.

Every forward-year report must include net portfolio CAGR, max drawdown, synthetic SPY CAGR and MDD, net annualized CAGR advantage in percentage points, capital utilization, SGOV/cash exposure, trade count (diagnostic only), abstentions, and performance by controlling niche and chromosome family. Objective: at least 20 percentage points annualized CAGR advantage over synthetic SPY, stretch 35 points, with portfolio MDD <=20%. Compare like-for-like capital, fees and calendar. Do not optimize trade frequency. Do not treat a single favorable synthetic year as proof of live transferability.

The model may use only information available by each decision cutoff. No future-year observations, fitted parameters, or outcomes may enter an earlier frozen evaluation. Synthetic data-generating processes must vary across independent five-year sets; report inability to reach the goal rather than weakening the return/risk thresholds.

Research implementation status: cross-family selection primitive and small functional replay tests exist. Native calibrated opportunity estimates, full evolutionary loop, five-year sequential runner, synthetic SPY comparison, and production certification are not yet established by these tests.
