# Independent Sector Leadership Research — v0.1
Purpose: Identify ex-ante differences among annual top-five, median-five, and bottom-five stocks per historical GICS sector.
Isolation: This repository must NOT read MTS DEV79, DEV37, DEV50, protected banks, chromosomes, rankings, or research results. Source new independently acquired point-in-time market, membership, sector and earnings data.
Years (frozen candidates): discovery 2013,2014,2017,2019; blind 2008,2011,2020,2022. Blind years are sealed until feature definitions and analysis are frozen.
Universe: historically eligible broad US equities, with PIT sector classification and inclusion/delisting handling. Never substitute today's S&P constituents.
Selection: annual total return ranks per sector, top five, five nearest median, bottom five; record eligibility counts and sector-year size. Descriptive cohorts are hindsight-defined and NOT trade signals.
Measures: point-in-time earnings announcement dates, earnings surprise, revenue surprise, guidance/revisions when available, 5/20/60/120-session stock vs sector and market excess return, breadth, volume, volatility and trend. Capture feature availability timestamps.
Outcomes: compare within-sector/year distributions, event-time pre/post earnings curves, market-adjusted excess return, sector beta and residual return. Use sector/year matched controls, effect sizes, uncertainty intervals and multiple-test adjustment.
Technical patterns: only investigate after primary relationships are quantified; pre-register any predictive rules before blind evaluation.
Blind tests: run the frozen rules without selecting winners using future returns. Report both descriptive annual cohorts and prospective portfolio-free predictive metrics separately.
No access to prior MTS data, no merging protected datasets, no Git remote or shared output directory until explicitly approved.
