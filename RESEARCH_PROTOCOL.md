## Frozen implementation conventions
H1 uses calendar month-end closes: close of month t-2 / close of month t-13 minus one (returns for months t-12 through t-2). H2 uses 252 sessions including formation. H5 is sample standard deviation of 252 daily simple returns (253 closes), annualized by sqrt(252). H5 primary ranks retain raw negative direction; gates, positive fractions and placebo statistics multiply IC by -1. Composite uses ranks H1,H3,H4 and rank(-H5), requires all four, and is ranked again within sector.
Stage1 chronological blocks: 2006–2008, 2009–2010, 2011–2013, 2014–2015. Primary family comprises twelve signal/horizon tests; Holm correction is reported. Missing calendar months invalidate HAC inference and gates. Deciles use ranks >0.9 and <=0.1; ties remain intact. Sparse or tied groups without both tails have missing spreads. Two legs each have unit gross weight; costs apply to the sum of absolute changes in both legs, including initial entry. H5 spread and holdings reverse direction. Months with broken continuity invalidate turnover inference.
No certified execution proxy. Missing next-session open or terminal close remains missing and is recorded. Sector medians require returns for every PIT member; otherwise the sector/month/horizon benchmark is unavailable. An explicit exchange-session calendar is required for certified use. Current panel-derived dates are exploratory only.
The executor is fail-closed: Stage1 requires verified source coverage before claiming its immutable once-only slot. Stage2 input access requires the logged complete Stage1 result and gate, matching configuration and claim hashes, and passing signal/horizon pairs. Identical interrupted runs may resume; changed configurations cannot rerun the claimed slot. Test fixtures are artificial inputs, never research data. EXECUTOR_V2.md documents the schema and local commands. Existing reports/smoke artifacts are exploratory/old protocol v1.
Lookahead t+1 signal versus return t is a warning diagnostic, not definitive evidence of leakage. Six-month decay is exploratory. Later variants require registration and an expanded multiple-test family before evaluation.
