# Frozen research protocol v1

Manifest frozen before this implementation inspects new-year outcomes. Discovery: 2011–2014; blind: 2015–2018. The latter are locked in the CLI. These are candidate non-crash years, not a claim of sufficient data or untouched outcomes outside this workspace. Previously seen 2019 is exploratory; 2020 is a separate crash pilot. Never optimize from either. Insufficient eight-year sourcing means incomplete, never substitute years after inspecting outcomes.

Monthly formation uses the last observed market session in the price panel. A production run requires a complete exchange calendar and panel; incomplete months invalidate formation. Universe intervals are start-inclusive/end-exclusive and contain stable security symbol, PIT sector and source. Security identifiers and ticker changes must be independently reconciled upstream; same ticker does not establish same issuer. Exits and delistings must be supplied. Current pilot selection is outcome-selected and cannot support inference.

H1 is adjusted close t−21 / t−252 minus one. H2 is close t / t−63 minus one. H3 is close t / highest adjusted close across 252 sessions. H4 is latest reported actual-minus-estimated EPS divided by sample standard deviation of prior eight quarterly errors, minimum four complete observations, zero dispersion means missing. H5 is annual gross profit divided by same-period-end assets, using the latest eligible period and filing vintage. Missing SEC tags remain missing. Filing/report dates must be strictly earlier than formation date, conservatively excluding same-day announcements without timestamps. Companyfacts restatements filed after formation cannot enter signals. FRED requires an as-of vintage; revised latest values are forbidden for historical regime analysis. Historical analyst revisions are unavailable, not assumed free or proxied by earnings surprises.

Signals use within-sector midrank percentiles. No raw-value winsorization: bounded ranks handle outliers, with ties retained. Equal-weight composite is secondary and requires all five ranks. Always report complete-case coverage and selection bias. Sparse sectors and tied extreme groups can produce unavailable quintile spreads; do not arbitrarily break ties.

Execution uses next observed adjusted open; missing open uses explicitly labeled next-close proxy. Yahoo adjusted open uses same-day adjusted-close/raw-close factor. This is provisional and is not proof of executable total returns. Horizon exit is last market close in calendar month t+1/3/6. Sector benchmark is equal-weight mean of observed eligible sector returns. Missing terminal observations are not zero returns and create documented survivor bias. Signal ranks freeze on the formation cross-section before terminal availability is examined. Terminal missingness still biases observed benchmark returns and metrics, so results are descriptive until a complete terminal-return panel exists.

Monthly sector Spearman IC, top-minus-bottom percentile quintile spread, top-half positive sector-excess hit rate, sector/year CSV breakdown, and long-top portfolio turnover are recorded. Turnover is sum absolute changes in equal-weight top holdings, first entry costs one, measured between available formations; missing months invalidate continuity. Costs are one-way 10/25 bps times this turnover, applied to top portfolio excess returns. This is a sensitivity calculation, not a trading claim; short-side implementation costs are not estimated. Aggregate IC equally weights sectors each month. Bartlett Newey-West lag h−1 handles overlapping horizons; normal-approximation p values are descriptive, especially in short samples. Holm adjusts all 15 primary signal/horizon tests jointly; composite is excluded. No pooled pseudo-independent stock or overlapping-horizon t tests. Missing monthly ICs can compromise lag spacing; do not infer significance from sparse series.

Stop predictive evaluation for missing certified PIT membership/sectors, unresolved identity, missing delisting/dividend treatment, insufficient history, unexplained adjusted-price discontinuities or calendar gaps, insufficient eight-year coverage, unlocked/contaminated holdout, or absent historical event availability. Diagnostics flag gaps longer than seven days and adjusted jumps ≥100% or ≤−50%; they do not repair or certify them. All current reports remain exploratory incomplete, even if code executes successfully.

Source order: PIT constituents first; Yahoo historical OHLCV provisional; SEC EDGAR companyfacts with filed dates; Alpha Vantage historical earnings surprises subject to entitlement; FRED/ALFRED regime vintages. No provider fallback without explicit provenance. API credentials stay in environment variables and never in output.

Implementation ETA: executable prototype in this session. Research completion has no defensible ETA until PIT universe, identities, delistings, source coverage and blind-year provenance are resolved. No predictive claim before valid holdout release under a separately reviewed protocol.
