## Rule
Only source-backed facts may be labeled GA0. Recollections are hypotheses until matched to preserved code/report/log evidence. Later G2/G3 structures may inform redesign but may not be back-projected into GA0.

## User-recalled GA0 behavior (UNVERIFIED SOURCE STATUS)
- approximately eight market-analysis tools/families;
- GA could evaluate them individually and splice/combine them;
- search was not recalled as having a fixed ~43k budget;
- GA sought integrated strategies and produced approximately four combined finalists ranked by CAGR/MDD;
- pushing MDD lower caused CAGR to collapse;
- architecture was attempting to find one best/universal strategy.

These statements are controlling archaeology targets, not yet code-proven facts.
