# MTS Layered Contextual Evolution Architecture v1
Date: 2026-10-07
Status: APPROVED WORKING ARCHITECTURE
Branch: research/layered-contextual-ga-redesign-20261007

## Purpose
Rebuild MTS as staged evolutionary research. GA splices semantically meaningful analytical genes into small chromosomes inside a hierarchical causal context. Each layer answers one scientific question, freezes promoted findings, and must pass transport before the next layer may use them.

CAGR and maximum drawdown are final system findings, not early evolutionary objectives.

Primary research objective: maximize credible prospective net expected value of each capital decision while independently preventing catastrophic loss.

## Context hierarchy
### Galaxy
Broad market context, initially represented by SPY for the S&P universe.
Descriptive state: STRONG_DOWN / DOWN / FLAT / UP / STRONG_UP.
Retain continuous causal measurements beneath the label: direction, strength, trajectory, volatility, breadth and other certified descriptors.

### Solar system
Sector context, independently described on the same five-state continuum.
Retain continuous descriptors and sector-relative-to-galaxy behavior.

### Planet
Individual stock. The planet is the trading object. It is not prohibited from being long because Galaxy or Solar System is down, nor prohibited from being short because they are up.

Galaxy and Solar System are context, not gates. GA may discover that agreement, disagreement, divergence or relative strength changes EV, uncertainty, MAE, lifecycle behavior or appropriate position size.

## Genetic vocabulary
A gene is a semantically meaningful analytical primitive/family.
A chromosome is a GA-discovered combination of genes, parameters and permitted interactions.
Context is observable information available to the chromosome; context does not automatically become a rule.

The exact original GA0 gene vocabulary MUST be recovered from preserved evidence before Stage 2 production search. Do not substitute remembered names and call them original.

Later G2 semantic family code is archaeological evidence only until provenance is established.

## Directionality
Stage 2 searches for predictable directional opportunity in both tails.
A LONG chromosome and SHORT chromosome may be exact or near mirrors, but symmetry is never imposed.
Exit-long does not imply enter-short. Exit-short does not imply enter-long. The opposite-direction chromosome must independently qualify.

## Layer rules
1. Every layer has a simple control.
2. Every layer freezes before the next layer consumes it.
3. No downstream result may silently retune an upstream frozen discovery.
4. Development fitness is insufficient; promotion requires transport.
5. Report raw N and effective independent N.
6. Complexity must earn itself through improved transported information.
7. No fixed holding period, fixed profit target or fixed percentage stop is presumed by the scientific layers.
8. Point-in-time causality is mandatory.
9. Protected governance banks remain protected. DEV50 is preserved-not-pristine and BLIND17 remains final blind unless governance is explicitly changed.
10. No portfolio CAGR/MDD optimization is allowed to leak into early discovery.

## Implementation stages

### Stage 0 — Archaeology and gene-library certification
Goal: recover GA0's actual analytical families, parameters, chromosome representation, crossover/mutation mechanics, objectives and observed search behavior.

Deliverables:
- evidence ledger with source path/commit for every recovered claim;
- certified gene registry;
- unresolved-items ledger;
- GA0 reconstruction control specification.

Gate: no production Stage-2 GA until gene provenance is certified or an explicit decision is made to create a new vocabulary rather than call it GA0.

### Stage 1 — Causal contextual map
Goal: describe Galaxy and Solar System without optimizing trades.

Outputs per causal decision time:
- five-state label;
- continuous strength;
- trajectory/change;
- volatility and certified contextual descriptors;
- sector-vs-Galaxy relationship.

The classifier describes the information available now. It must not use future returns to choose today's state.

Gate: PIT/leakage tests, deterministic replay, boundary/stability characterization and coverage tests pass.

### Stage 2 — Planet directional-opportunity discovery
Goal: discover small chromosomes identifying positive credible prospective net EV for LONG or SHORT opportunities given Planet characteristics and optional Galaxy/Solar-System context.

Explicit exclusions:
- no CAGR objective;
- no MDD objective;
- no position sizing;
- no portfolio construction;
- no lifecycle/exit optimization;
- no arbitrary holding-period gene.

GA may add/remove/substitute analytical genes and contextual interactions. Complexity begins bounded and can expand only after ablation evidence.

Forward paths are measured across multiple causal horizons to characterize where an edge appears, peaks and decays; horizon is a finding, not a pre-imposed trade duration.

Output: ranked, frozen directional opportunity chromosome bank with discovery EV, uncertainty, frequency, path statistics and provenance.

Gate: transported credible net EV and independent support.

### Stage 3 — Context contribution / ablation
Compare frozen discovery under:
- Planet only;
- Planet + Galaxy;
- Planet + Solar System;
- Planet + Galaxy + Solar System.

Also test interaction/divergence effects. Context earns inclusion by improving transported information, uncertainty or path characterization.

### Stage 4 — Entry and MAE
Consume frozen Stage-2 opportunities.
Question: enter immediately or wait for market-determined confirmation?

Study successful vs failed forward paths and normal MAE. Discover causal confirmation characteristics; quantify avoided losers/MAE against lost winners/EV caused by waiting.

No fixed waiting period.

### Stage 5 — Thesis failure
Consume frozen opportunity + entry rules.
Question: what observable evidence indicates the original assessment is no longer valid?

Learn distinction between normal MAE and failure-like deterioration. Compare learned criteria with simple controls such as fixed/ATR stops, without presuming those controls are optimal.

Catastrophe protection is separate and may override this layer.

### Stage 6 — Position lifecycle
Research market-determined ADD / HOLD / REDUCE / EXIT decisions using remaining prospective EV and current evidence.
Duration is an outcome.
Initially research action questions separately before permitting a combined lifecycle policy.

### Stage 7 — Capital competition
Compare incumbents, challengers and SAFE using prospective net EV after costs and uncertainty.
Replacement requires enough incremental credible EV to justify turnover.
A dead long thesis does not automatically create a short; opposite direction independently qualifies.

### Stage 8 — Position sizing
Determine exposure from credible EV, uncertainty, expected MAE/tail behavior, Galaxy/Solar-System support or opposition, correlation and concentration.
Broad/sector context influences size and vigilance; it is not a hard invest/do-not-invest gate.

### Stage 9 — Portfolio and catastrophe defense
Combine simultaneous planets while controlling correlated exposures, sector concentration, liquidity and systemic risk.
Integrate the separately governed A/B/C crash-defense and recovery architecture only here unless a prior layer explicitly needs its causal descriptors.
Catastrophe defense has survival override authority.

### Stage 10 — Full-system report card
Only after Stages 0–9 are frozen and integrated calculate:
- CAGR;
- MDD;
- terminal wealth;
- CVaR/tail loss;
- worst day/week/month;
- turnover/costs;
- capital utilization;
- recovery;
- crash behavior;
- concentration;
- era/transport stability.

These are findings. If a result is poor, diagnose the responsible layer rather than retuning early chromosomes directly against CAGR/MDD.

## Required scientific comparisons
- Planet-only vs hierarchical context.
- Single genes vs combinations.
- Marginal gene contribution.
- LONG vs SHORT symmetry/asymmetry.
- Immediate entry vs learned confirmation.
- Learned thesis failure vs simple stop controls.
- Frozen discovery EV vs transported EV and transfer ratio.
- Raw observation count vs effective independent support.
- Simple baseline vs evolved layer at every promotion gate.

## Search philosophy
Use GA0-like semantic compression: evolution combines meaningful market-analysis concepts rather than rediscovering finance from huge recursive primitive trees.

Do not pre-author the interpretation of Galaxy/Solar-System states. For example, a strong Planet in a weak Sector may be exceptional relative strength or excessive risk; the data must decide.

Do not create a monster chromosome containing opportunity, entry, stop, lifecycle, allocation and portfolio behavior. Each layer evolves only what is necessary for its question.

## Numerical parameters intentionally NOT frozen
Population, generations/search budget, maximum chromosome length, exact state boundaries, EV estimator, uncertainty estimator, forward-path grid, promotion thresholds, bootstrap count and complexity penalties require calibration experiments. They may not be silently chosen and frozen by implementation.
