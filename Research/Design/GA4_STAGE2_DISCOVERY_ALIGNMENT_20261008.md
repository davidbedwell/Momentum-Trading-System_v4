# GA4 Stage 2 — alignment to frozen ten-stage conveyor
Date: 2026-10-08
Authority: Research/Design/MTS_AUTONOMOUS_RESEARCH_CONVEYOR_V1_20261007.md
Status: IMPLEMENTATION ALIGNMENT; NOT SCIENTIFIC CERTIFICATION

- Stage 2 discovers a broad, nonredundant opportunity bank. No 1–20 cap, forced regime ranking, portfolio optimization, or final selection quota.
- Discovery uses semantic genes, separate LONG/SHORT evidence, small initial chromosomes, causal context at decision time t, and no downstream lifecycle/sizing/portfolio fitness.
- The GA's population size is a computational search parameter, not a limit on archived qualifying discoveries.
- Stage 2 promotions require the frozen evidence contract: credible net EV, uncertainty, effective N, recurrence, null comparison, transport and complexity contribution. Passing Stage 2 is not final validation.
- Stage 3 ablates market/sector/stock context. Stages 4–9 investigate entry, failure, lifecycle, capital, sizing and catastrophic risk. Stage 10 reports outcomes; it does not silently tune upstream genes.
- Candidate identity is permutation-invariant for conjunctions of semantic chromosomes; thresholds/genes remain part of identity. A novelty exhaustion must stop explicitly, never silently admit duplicate candidates.
- DEV80 only for current discovery. DEV37, DEV50, BLIND17 and verification banks remain protected; no heldout-driven repair.
- No production GA launched by this alignment. Preflight, causal checks, calibration, frozen risk policy, scientific gates, backups and durability verification remain required before production.
