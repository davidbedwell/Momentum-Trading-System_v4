# Stage 1 / Stage 2 continuous context policy

Stage 1 owns point-in-time continuous Galaxy, Sector and Planet descriptors. Five-state labels are descriptive metadata only, not mandatory GA strata, eligibility gates, fitness objectives or quotas.

Stage 2 GA may discover continuous thresholds and interactions within and across broad labels. Retain the broad provisional strategy bank, 63-horizon evidence, uncertainty and provenance; no early 1–20 cap.

Stage 3 tests incremental Galaxy and Sector context. Later AI analysis may discover new groupings, requiring independent validation. Stage 9 integrates separately frozen A/B/C and R-1.

Stage 1 PASS alone does not establish Stage 2 linkage: require source hashes, decision-time alignment, coverage and missingness audits. DEV80 design only; protected banks untouched.
