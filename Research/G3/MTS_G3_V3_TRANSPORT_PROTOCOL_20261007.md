# G3 V3 TRANSPORT PROTOCOL — PROSPECTIVE FREEZE — 2026-10-07

Transport is authorized only after MTS records a V3 inferential PASS (>=2/3 nulls).
The transport universe is the historical DEV117 reconstructed solely from the four frozen 2026-10-06 DEV80 train membership artifacts. For each fold, blind37 = DEV117 minus that fold's DEV80. No outcome data are used to reconstruct membership.
Candidates are frozen V3 Real-arm finalists recovered deterministically from the exact gate seeds/code; no mutation, repair, threshold change, or lifecycle change is permitted after gate completion.
Each candidate is evaluated unchanged on each of the four blind37 folds using the same G3 causal features, applicability predicate, lifecycle and 10 bps cost semantics.
Transport is descriptive cross-stock validation; folds share market history and are not treated as four independent market episodes.
Promotion rule: candidate must have >=25 total firing events in each fold, positive mean net event return in at least 3 of 4 folds, and positive event-count-weighted pooled mean across all four folds. Otherwise reject.
No candidate may be selected or modified using transport outcomes.
If zero candidates promote, G3 transport FAILS and final report records no transport-validated specialist.
If >=1 promotes, G3 transport PASSES and final G3 report may characterize the promoted specialist bank. Portfolio allocation remains out of scope.
Missing data/artifacts or inability to reproduce DEV117/37 membership => STOP_FAIL_CLOSED.
