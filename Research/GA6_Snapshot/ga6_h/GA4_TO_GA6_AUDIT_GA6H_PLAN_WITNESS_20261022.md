# GA4 → GA6 Evidence Audit and GA6-H Research Plan — Draft for Independent Witness
Date: 2026-10-22. Status: engineering plan, NOT certified research. Scope: GA6-H independently predicts/ranks US equities for 1–40 trading sessions. No GA6 niche genome, evolved elite, or GA4 winning chromosome is imported as a seed.

## Executive decision
Transfer proven infrastructure and testable design ideas, not claimed GA4→GA6 performance uplift. No available matched GA4-versus-GA6 controlled experiment isolates a particular genetic modification and proves it raised out-of-sample CAGR. Treat all purported uplift as unproven until ablated under a common evaluator.

## Transfer matrix
A. KEEP / infrastructure with parity tests: next-open fills, fees, corporate actions, settlement, cash/NAV, PIT membership, guarded evaluator, time-causal feature cache, checkpoint/RNG resume, parallel worker scheduling, immutable hashes, testable validation governance. Prefer adapting existing tested GA5/GA6 implementation over simplified rewrites.
B. KEEP / discovery capabilities but re-specify for rankings: 8+ families (momentum/acceleration, trend geometry, volume/liquidity, volatility, relative strength, sector, breadth, PIT earnings), nonlinear AND/OR/grouped expressions, continuous transforms, cross-family recombination, Pareto multiobjective diversity, sample-support and time-block uncertainty, stagnation and novelty. These expand search expressiveness; NO proven causal performance lift yet.
C. ADAPT / GA6 trade-first EV: evaluate top-k *relative* future performance and executable replacement-vs-cash net opportunity, after costs and exposure. Keep downside tail, hit rate, calibration, opportunity coverage, trade count, duration, turnover. Do not optimize EV alone; portfolio CAGR/MDD are independent out-of-sample gates.
D. ADAPT / exit genes: fixed 1–40 session max; GA may evolve 1–40 horizon, earlier profit/stop/condition exits, trailing stops, hold-vs-replace. A horizon label is NOT an exit. Validate each with matched entries and cash accounting.
E. ADAPT / regime: market/sector/breadth and defensive risk information are optional *features* or portfolio risk overlays, not GA6 niche membership and not a compulsory three-state classifier.
F. DO NOT TRANSFER: GA4/GA6 evolved genomes/elites, historical winners as seed, niche quotas or retrospective niche labels, look-ahead regime boundaries, future earnings dates without first-known timestamp, EV-to-CAGR extrapolation, fixed risk interventions that sacrifice return without matched benefit, unverified GA6-H daily simulator as production engine.

## Research freeze: objective and constraints
Primary objective: independently evolve daily cross-sectional stock opportunity scores to select stocks likely to outperform over the next 1–40 exchange sessions; target >=35% portfolio CAGR with controlled maximum drawdown, not a promise. Compare to prior 25%+ development benchmark using identical stock dates, costs, exposures and portfolio accounting.
Initial capital $100,000; typical 3% position, at most 10% per strong opportunity; 10bps comparable costs; next-open orders only; maximum 40 exchange sessions held; allow cash/no trade; protective exits permitted at any time. Quarterly disclosure: no new entry or position increase from two exchange sessions before through two after mapped disclosure session. Require timestamped first-publication schedules known at decision time; if unverified, fail closed and report coverage. Never use today's EODHD earnings archive as historical publication evidence.
Strict chronological decision features, lagged market/sector data, no future label leakage. Historical price adjustments, split/dividend handling and delisted securities require provenance. Current 4,967 symbols are survivor-biased; do not claim PIT certification or live-like unbiased CAGR without delisted/PIT coverage.

## Engineering phases and acceptance gates
0. Preserve GA6 and GA4 artifacts; produce immutable hashes, versions, exact evaluator differences, experiment lineage and original 25%+ CAGR portfolio run identification. No changes to running GA6.
1. GA4→GA6 attribution audit: inventory feature/gene/operator/fitness/exits/portfolio changes; identify each changed component by source and date; recreate matched GA4 baseline and GA6 variant with same universe, periods, risk, cost, data, execution, seed budget and held-out splits. Ablate one component at a time plus factorial interactions; report delta CAGR, delta MDD, opportunity hit-rate, trade count, uncertainty, costs and out-of-sample decay. Without comparable replay mark 'unproven', never 'positive'.
2. Repair GA6-H research engine: reuse parity-tested GA6/GA5 execution, not simplified provisional simulator; enforce next-open and prior-close marks, mandatory exit queue, splits/dividends, holidays, stale prices, symbol mapping, transaction ledger, settlement, liquidity and capacity, no borrow/short until separately approved. Full invariant and property tests.
3. Data audit: ticker mapping, invalid bars and corporate actions, universe survivorship and delisted history; provenance/known-at ledger for EODHD quarterly announcements; row-level quarantine. Determine which historical periods have sufficient verified earnings schedules. Never silently bypass embargo to create trades.
4. Baselines: random eligible, momentum/reversal, sector-relative, volatility-adjusted, SPY and cash proxy, same execution. Evaluate chronological folds, costs, capacity and regime episodes. Keep all diagnostic outcomes.
5. GA chromosome design: score grammar with feature-family transforms, window 1–252 where causal, cross-sectional normalization on date t, interactions, AND/OR/grouped clauses, missing-data logic, risk/volatility adjustment, ranking aggregator, exit and hold genes (1–40). Avoid arbitrary gene count cap; budgeted complexity and novelty penalties, cross-family coverage and mutation/crossover. Do not include ticker identity or future outcomes as genes.
6. Fitness: multiple objectives for top-k net excess forward outcome, rank IC/calibration, time-block confidence lower bound, tail downside, coverage, turnover, exposure and portfolio capacity. No scalar 'best chromosome' as sole gate; maintain Pareto archive and diversity. Purge overlapping 1–40-session labels; control multiple comparisons and record every candidate tried.
7. GA production engineering: 8-worker scaling benchmark, shared immutable feature cache, process memory, deterministic seeds, checkpoint/resume parity, reproducible artifact lineage, generation/evaluation counters, stagnation with measured novelty and user-approved run budget. GA6 runs concurrently only when resource-safe.
8. Portfolio selection: each close t rank all *eligible* candidates, choose executable top opportunities or cash; next-open fills, 3% default/10% max risk, exposure/correlation/sector concentration measured rather than arbitrary hard quotas; evolve adaptive entry/exit/replacement only after baseline. Include failed fills and cost.
9. Validation: development-only evolution; rolling purged walk-forward and stock-disjoint transport; separate 2018/2020/2022 episode tests; protected A/B blind untouched; no repurposing previously seen data as pristine. Repeat seeds and confidence intervals. Survivorship limitation prominently disclosed.
10. Final promotion: report CAGR, MDD, Calmar, hit rate, EV, average/maximum hold, turnover, fees, liquidity, exposure, drawdown episodes, statistical uncertainty, fold dispersion, benchmark lift and causal data coverage. >=35% CAGR target must not override robustness. Certification only if data integrity, PIT, execution and untouched tests pass. Paper trade before live capital.

## Independent witness protocol
Give Claude or Grok this exact packet and source evidence. Ask for adversarial review, not agreement. Specifically: (1) Which GA4→GA6 improvements are actually causally demonstrated? (2) Identify leakage/selection/survivorship and future-aware earnings problems. (3) Audit full portfolio execution incl. corporate actions, exits, sizing and timing. (4) Challenge GA fitness, Pareto metrics and cross-validation. (5) Identify missing scientific gates and overengineering. (6) Return blockers, critical defects, recommended tests and minimal correction order. Explicitly distinguish proven, plausible and unsupported claims. Do not give the reviewer credentials, secrets, private account data or untouched blind holdings.
Acceptance: witness response stored verbatim with model ID, request hash, UTC time, cited evidence and review disposition. Independent review does not substitute for executable tests.
