# MTS evolutionary mutation governance — 2026-10-10

Status: engineering decision, not independent scientific certification.

1. Gen1, Gen2 and the Gen3 pilot are immutable historical evidence. Retain exact genome definitions, raw evaluations, checksums and provenance.
2. New descendants may mutate numerical threshold/quantile/severity genes continuously **only within the original certified gene-specific extrema**, precision 0.001. Categorical feature IDs and enum choices remain restricted to the certified catalog.
3. Historical genomes outside these bounds remain in the research library, marked DOMAIN_EXCEPTION_HISTORICAL. Do not silently repair or erase them; do not use them as direct breeding parents until an independently justified domain amendment is approved. This rule applies irrespective of measured EV.
4. Do not extend bounds based on the in-sample returns of Gen2 children. A domain amendment requires ex-ante scientific rationale, a new protocol version, and a separate experimental cohort.
5. A positive exploratory LCB is not a calibrated post-search confidence statement. Promotion to live use requires multiplicity-aware evaluation, independent episodes and risk/cost checks.
6. Diversity must include both genome structure and actual point-in-time trade-signal overlap; two genetically different chromosomes that trade identically are redundant for library admission.
7. Compute budgets and stopping rules must be frozen before the next production run; report discovered specialists by side, horizon, and behavior rather than a single pooled winner.
8. Generation-3 pilot output belongs to the old runner and is not certified. Start a distinct versioned run for the redesigned engine. No automatic 200-generation launch before integration, restart/replay tests and a fixed-budget pilot pass.
