# Gen1 matched recovery/failure study — original horizon scope freeze

- Population: **all 4,360 original Gen1 genomes**, not only the 168 provisional parents.
- Eligible original terminal horizons only: **1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 63 trading sessions**.
- **Exclude** Phase B extended candidate horizons (64–126), Phase C fine-grained candidate horizons (1–126), and all derived intermediate-horizon optimization results from horizon selection, comparison, and conclusions in this study.
- Daily prices **inside** a selected original horizon may be used to observe distress/recovery and to construct checkpoint labels; they are not additional candidate holding horizons.
- Do not impose universal 20-session terminal labels. Select/freeze a genome's horizon using training-only evidence; assess only checkpoints strictly earlier than that horizon. No later checkpoint for horizon 1.
- Original all-candidate horizon metrics may contain missing/unsupported values; retain those genomes in the population with an explicit insufficient-evidence status, not an invented horizon.
- Hindsight may label paths; held-out predictors must remain point-in-time and have verified release availability.
- Scope DEV80 only. DEV37, DEV50 and BLIND17 protected. Freeze 168 provisional parents; no Gen2 promotion.
- Prior universal-20-session pilot evidence remains exploratory and cannot certify or promote any genome.
