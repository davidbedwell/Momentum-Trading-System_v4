# MTS Horizon GA — Alpha Discovery Scope (2026-10-10)

Decision: Crash-specific eligibility, promotion, and parent-selection gates are OUT OF SCOPE for the 13 holding-window generational GA.

- Preserve all historical observations, including GFC, COVID and 2022, in existing DEV80 data. Do not remove or censor crash-era rows.
- Do not use the separate market-episode diagnostic results as parent-selection or promotion vetoes.
- Evaluate each genome on its intended window: 2–5, 6–10, 11–15, 16–20, 21–25, 26–30, 31–35, 36–40, 41–45, 46–50, 51–55, 56–60, 61–65.
- Retain net EV, prospective costs, uncertainty, effective sample size, ordinary trade-tail loss and genetic/signal diversity as discovery criteria.
- Report multiple-comparison uncertainty and avoid claiming independent validation from DEV80.
- DEV37 remains transport evaluation, DEV50 preserved and untouched, BLIND17 final blind; no automatic access to any heldout.
- Crash etiology, systemic risk detectors, defensive regime tactics and recovery logic belong to a separate future research track.
- All resulting parent lists are provisional until selection rules are frozen and required evidence gates met.
