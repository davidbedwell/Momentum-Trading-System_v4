## Allocation
- Preserve all 4,360 candidate records and all 977 preliminary EV-positive distinct chromosomes; do not delete unselected genomes.
- Target exactly **168 distinct parent genomes**, not 168 window memberships, subject to scientific certification and availability of 168 qualifying distinct genomes. Never fill slots with scientifically ineligible genomes.
- Horizon windows: 1–3, 4–7, 8–15, 16–30, 31–45, 46–63 sessions. A 64–126 window awaits Phase B and requires separate user authorization before changing this frozen allocation.
- Include **all 21 currently eligible distinct 1–3-session genomes** as the first allocation (subject to certification; any failure must be reported rather than silently replaced).
- Select **29 NEW distinct genomes** from each of the five other populated windows (145 additional parents). A genome already allocated in an earlier window cannot fill a new distinct-genome slot, but retains all window memberships and diagnostics. Evaluate the windows in ascending horizon order for deterministic slot accounting.
- Allocate the final **two** positions to underrepresented genetically distinct, scientifically eligible lineages among the remaining genomes, with their eligible window recorded. Do not use arbitrary fill candidates.
- This is a **population budget**, not evidence that 168 parents are viable, diverse, or optimal. Any certification shortfall or inadequate diversity triggers a report and reapproval, not automatic relaxation.

## Selection and ranking governance
- Within each horizon window, rank eligible chromosome-window memberships using successive multidimensional Pareto fronts, not a cross-window global leaderboard or single weighted score.
- Allowed ranking axes: net expected value; reliability/statistical confidence adjusted for dependent observations and multiple horizon searches; consistency across actually measured neighboring horizons. Evaluate genetic/behavioral diversity to avoid duplicate lineages and preserve family/side representation.
- For selection within a Pareto front, prefer demonstrably distinct genomes/behaviors; record ties, objective values, chosen window, skipped duplicates, and selection reason. No arbitrary genetic-distance threshold is approved yet.
- MAE, tail MAE, EV/MAE, and matched passive/excess EV remain **diagnostics only**, with zero eligibility/rank/selection weight under existing freezes. Trade frequency, regime behavior and sample size are diagnostics or reliability inputs only where scientifically justified; no undisclosed extra gate.
- The provisional 2026-10-09 ranking based on best-positive-horizon EV, its LCB95, and positive-horizon fraction is NOT a certified selection rubric: best-horizon selection and dependent tests introduce bias. Do not promote its ranks to parent decisions without predeclared multiplicity/reliability and diversity rules.
- Record candidate quality by window, side, genetic family combination, sample support, and measured horizon behavior; retain rejected/skipped candidates with reasons.
