# Crash cash-first, independent opportunity reevaluation — user clarification

Status: USER APPROVED; supersedes any interpretation of the cash-first rule as a permanent ban on trading during Defensive.

1. On an A/B/C crash detection, FIRST action is a transition toward cash: cancel conflicting pending entries and initiate executable risk-managed liquidation of existing equity exposure. Do not assume instantaneous fills or no gap/slippage. Do not immediately replace exposures with SHORTs.
2. AFTER the cash-first action, reevaluate any proposed LONG or SHORT opportunity independently against the current market regime, point-in-time signal, expected net edge, costs, borrow constraints (SHORT), risk, and explicit Defensive-state eligibility. No position is automatically reinstated.
3. Any subsequent Defensive trade requires a separately authorized, scientifically validated Defensive strategy and portfolio risk limits; independent reevaluation does not itself grant permission to trade. Existing R-1 recovery remains the gate for restoring ordinary Normal trading.
4. SHORT discovery is not restricted to crashes, and crash detection does not itself generate a SHORT entry. The frozen 6% annualized borrow-cost model and maximum actual quoted rate remain in force.
5. Preserve protected-bank rules, frozen Gen1 parent allocation, and scientific certification requirements. This amendment is governance, not proof the controller has been modified or tested.
