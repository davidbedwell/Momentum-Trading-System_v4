## Evaluation philosophy
Use broad historical stress taxonomy rather than only three answer-key crashes. Evaluate:
- rapid systemic crises,
- severe corrections that did not become full crashes,
- slow/grinding bears that A may legitimately miss,
- ordinary volatility spikes,
- calm regimes.

Primary questions:
1. Does the mechanism activate for the same economic/market reason across episodes?
2. Is behavior stable under modest parameter perturbation?
3. Does it avoid dependence on one exact threshold or conjunction?
4. Does it transport leave-episode-out?
5. Are alerts concentrated in objectively severe systemic deterioration rather than ordinary market noise?
6. Does it add complementary coverage to B/C rather than duplicate them?

## Selection
