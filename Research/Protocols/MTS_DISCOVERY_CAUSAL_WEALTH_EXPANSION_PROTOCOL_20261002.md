# MTS v4 — Discovery Causal Wealth Expansion Protocol — 2026-10-02

## Objective
Test the original capital-allocation principle as a wealth-accumulation hypothesis: when causal pre-entry evidence identifies unusually attractive opportunities, MTS may expand portfolio exposure rather than being constrained to equal-sizing exposure.

## Scientific distinction
Exposure-matched equal sizing remains a diagnostic control for allocation efficiency. It is NOT the objective function and is NOT a permanent constraint. The primary question is terminal wealth generated by a causal allocation policy, interpreted jointly with CAGR, drawdown, CVaR, exposure, concentration, and breadth.

## Evidence boundary
Candidate generation uses Discovery Development only. Existing Discovery Holdout remains unavailable until representative candidate policies are prospectively frozen. No additional Verification A and no Verification B access.

## Fixed predictive input
Use the already-replicated causal EV/p10 score: historical expected return divided by historical p10 downside, with only outcomes completed strictly before signal date; minimum N=100; genome -> family -> global -> unavailable backoff. Do not alter the predictor in this experiment.

## Candidate policy families
1. Absolute score boost-only: baseline 1.0x, higher causal EV/p10 may receive additional capital.
2. Same-day relative opportunity boost: stronger contemporaneous EV/p10 opportunities receive additional capital; weaker opportunities retain baseline capital.
3. Opportunity-density expansion: total permitted portfolio heat may increase when multiple high-ranked opportunities coexist.
4. Hybrid quality x density expansion: per-trade multiplier and total heat respond jointly but monotonically to causal opportunity quality/density.

## Broad search only
Use coarse multipliers/caps sufficient to reveal a response surface, including maximum per-trade multipliers 1.10x, 1.25x, 1.50x, 1.75x, and 2.00x where simulator constraints permit. Same-day top fractions: 25% and 50%. Density states must be simple and interpretable. No fine-grained threshold optimization.

## Evaluation
For every policy report terminal wealth, CAGR, max drawdown, CVaR5, average and peak gross exposure, minimum cash, return/drawdown, positive ticker/year/family breadth, ticker/genome contribution concentration, and ticker-cluster bootstrap CI. Also report ordinary equal-sizing and exposure-matched equal-sizing controls.

## Wealth/risk frontier
Do not reject a candidate merely because both max drawdown and CVaR are numerically worse than equal sizing. Instead identify Pareto/non-dominated policies across terminal wealth, max drawdown, and CVaR5, and report explicit incremental wealth versus incremental risk. A policy is scientifically interesting only if gains are broad and not concentration-driven. Catastrophic or obviously disproportionate risk escalation remains disqualifying.

## Development advancement
Require positive mean contribution, >=60% positive tickers, >=60% positive years, >=60% positive families, ticker-cluster 95% CI lower bound >0, and <=15% positive-contribution concentration in one ticker or genome. Require neighboring/coarser policies to support the same directional relationship. Representative frontier policies, not the single historical maximum, may be frozen for Holdout.

## Integrity
No A3 outcome is used to fit numeric parameters. A3 motivates the architecture question only. No Discovery Holdout/A/B access during candidate generation. No promotion to paper/live trading from Development.
