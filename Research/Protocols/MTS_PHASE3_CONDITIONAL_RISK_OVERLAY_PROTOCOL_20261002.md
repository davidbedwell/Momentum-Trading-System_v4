# MTS Phase 3 — Conditional Risk Overlay Protocol — 2026-10-02

## Frozen question
Can a causal, state-conditioned exposure controller reduce drawdown of the functioning Phase-3 opportunity engine without destroying its return advantage?

## Evidence boundary
Use only the already-consumed 67-stock development lab and the already-frozen Phase-3 chromosomes/results. No remaining Discovery or Verification bank may be accessed.

## Base opportunity engines
Freeze the Phase-3 opportunity chromosomes before this overlay. The controller may change exposure only; it may not alter qualification, ranking, evidence policy, or competition genes.

## Warning mechanism
Use the replicated core warning from the frozen signal-meaning state matrix: extreme MTS signal excitement coincident with explosive volatility expansion. Excitement alone, negative acceleration alone, or downtrend alone must not trigger protection.

Because the original state-matrix daily breadth/SPY state tape was not retained as a reusable artifact, this experiment tests the replicated core interaction only. It must not silently substitute a different breadth proxy.

## Causal state construction
At each session, use information available through the prior close only.
- Excitement: new-signal count relative to its expanding 60-session/history distribution; test extreme cutoffs at 90th and 95th percentile.
- Volatility expansion: 20-session realized volatility / 60-session realized volatility of the frozen base portfolio return stream; warning threshold fixed at 1.5, matching the prior comparator.
- Warning requires BOTH extreme excitement and volatility ratio >= 1.5.

## Controller genes
- warning exposure: 75%, 50%, 25%, 0%
- confirmation: 1, 2, 3 sessions
- recovery hysteresis after warning clears: 0, 2, 5, 10 sessions
- excitement percentile: 90%, 95%
No leverage. Removed equity exposure moves to 3% annualized safe asset.

## Evaluation
Replay 0/5/10/15/20 bps. Report CAGR, terminal wealth, max drawdown, CVaR5, worst day, safe fraction, warning fraction, switches, and four-era CAGR. Advancement requires all four eras positive at every tested cost.

Pareto controls advancement on terminal wealth/CAGR, max drawdown, CVaR5, worst day, and turnover. Do not select merely for lower drawdown or more time in safe.

## Target
Primary research target: determine whether worst-grid drawdown can be pushed below 30%, and preferably toward 25%, while preserving materially more CAGR than the qualification-only <=25% solutions (~7% CAGR). This target is diagnostic, not a forced optimization constraint.

## Governance
This is development-lab mechanism research, not deployment validation. Current-S&P survivorship bias remains. Untouched banks remain sealed.
