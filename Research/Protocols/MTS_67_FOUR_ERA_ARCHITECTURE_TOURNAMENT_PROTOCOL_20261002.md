## Common comparison conditions
- Same eligible 67-stock opportunity stream and portable-genome filter.
- Same causal information boundary.
- $100,000 reset each era.
- 3% annualized return on genuinely unallocated/safe capital.
- No leverage; maximum equity exposure 100%.
- Report 0, 10, 25 and 50 bps cost sensitivity.
- Preserve each architecture's actual allocation mechanics; do not rewrite old arms to resemble new slot architectures or vice versa.

## Required comprehensive outputs
For every strategy × era × cost:
- ending equity, total return, CAGR;
- maximum drawdown, CVaR5, worst day;
- positive-return/upside capture and negative-return/downside capture versus same-era EQUAL baseline;
- turnover, estimated transaction cost, switches/reallocations where defined;
- average/min/max safe fraction and average equity exposure;
- trade/signal counts, accepted/funded/rejected counts where defined;
- family participation/concentration;
- winner retention and loser avoidance for qualification architectures;
- rejection reason decomposition: evidence, positive-probability, p10 downside, EV/safe hurdle, uncertainty adjustment;
- crisis versus non-crisis behavior where observable;
- four-era aggregate, worst-era and dispersion/stability summaries.

## Scientific interpretation
No single headline return determines success. Compare return retained per unit of harmful behavior removed, robustness across eras, cost sensitivity, capital utilization, and mechanism. Pareto remains the interpretive framework; this tournament does not retune the frozen chromosomes.
