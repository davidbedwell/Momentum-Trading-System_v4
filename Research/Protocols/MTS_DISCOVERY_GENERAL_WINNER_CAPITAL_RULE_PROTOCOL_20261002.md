# MTS v4 Discovery — General Winner Capital Rule Protocol — 2026-10-02

## Objective
Discover a causal prospective capital-allocation rule that generalizes across MTS winners rather than deriving aggregate wealth from a small number of tickers. The prior frozen EV/p10 sizing rule is evidence/motivation, not a privileged answer.

## Evidence boundaries
Discovery only. Use canonical corrected Discovery population. Verification A2 outcomes may be used only for the separate 2-vs-18 mechanism audit; they may NOT be used to fit, select, threshold, or rank new general-rule candidates. The sequestered Verification-A 60 and all Verification B remain inaccessible.

## Two tracks
1. **Mechanism audit:** explain the frozen A2 2-positive/18-negative ticker breadth result descriptively. No optimization from A2.
2. **General-rule Discovery:** search only Discovery data for causal pre-entry evidence associated with subsequent winner quality and capital-sizing value.

## Candidate dimensions
No indicator is privileged. Start with available causal dimensions and scientifically motivated interactions: genome/family historical expectancy and downside-tail risk; evidence count/maturity; historical win rate and winner magnitude; reward/risk asymmetry; pre-entry volatility/ATR state; trend/structure/path variables already present causally in the research cache; family/genome/ticker/year/regime context where available. New derived features must be computable using information available by the prospective entry decision.

## Search objective
Terminal wealth is NOT sufficient. Candidate ranking must reward generalization. At minimum report:
- mean sizing contribution;
- terminal compounded wealth/CAGR under matched portfolio assumptions;
- max drawdown and CVaR5;
- positive ticker breadth and contribution concentration;
- positive year breadth;
- positive family breadth;
- positive genome breadth;
- ticker-cluster uncertainty;
- regime stability when a causal regime field can be constructed without leakage.

Prefer broad plateaus/stable relationships over isolated optima. No candidate may advance merely because a few names dominate aggregate wealth.

## Advancement gate
A candidate can become a Discovery nominee only if all are true on the Development selection sample:
1. positive mean sizing contribution;
2. positive contribution in >=60% of eligible tickers;
3. positive contribution in >=60% of eligible years;
4. positive contribution in >=60% of eligible signal families;
5. no single ticker supplies >15% of total positive contribution;
6. no single genome supplies >15% of total positive contribution;
7. ticker-cluster bootstrap 95% CI for mean sizing contribution has lower bound >0;
8. portfolio wealth exceeds equal sizing without materially worsening both max drawdown and CVaR5;
9. neighboring/simple parameterizations show the same directional relationship; no isolated optimum.

These are Discovery nomination standards, not post-hoc Validation rescue criteria.

## Holdout
Discovery Holdout remains unavailable during candidate generation/selection. Only after candidate(s) are frozen may the existing Discovery Holdout be replayed according to a separately frozen replay manifest. No Verification evidence is consumed by this experiment.

## Scientific authority
Analysis Engine performs deterministic calculations only. Interpretation, hypothesis formation, anomaly assessment, and decisions remain with the AI Research Director under MTS governance.
