# GA4 — Conditional Trading Relationship Research Charter
**Status:** USER-APPROVED SCIENTIFIC INTENT — 2026-10-08
**Branch:** `research/ga4-conditional-relationships`
**Authority:** User approval of reconstructed ten-stage architecture. This is a new memorialization, **not** a recovered original ten-stage freeze.

## Governing distinction
G0 attempted to optimize one global trading strategy using CAGR and maximum drawdown (MDD). GA4 MUST NOT repeat that design. GA4 is a multi-dimensional evolutionary **research and relationship discovery engine**. Its primary output is an auditable, searchable catalog of conditional trading behaviors, including useful non-global winners. CAGR and MDD are downstream portfolio outcomes, not universal early-stage discovery fitness or filters.

## Ten research stages (reconstructed and approved)
1. **Causal context:** market, sector/formal PIT taxonomy where valid, causal dynamic peers, and stock environments.
2. **Chromosome discovery:** explore potentially thousands of chromosomes and 1–4 chromosome combinations, including interactions, over DEV80; preserve diverse candidate evidence.
3. **Conditional relationships:** identify where chromosomes and combinations work, fail, or change behavior by environment; ablate context and distinguish genuine predictive relationships from identity leakage.
4. **Forward trajectories:** record the complete day-by-day forward response for horizons 1 through 63 inclusive, not just selected endpoints.
5. **EV and MAE:** measure conditional expected economic value, maximum adverse excursion, risk and their joint relationships across environments and all 63 horizons. Costs and causal availability must be explicit.
6. **Entry and thesis failure:** discover entry quality, invalidation, failed recovery, and deterioration conditions.
7. **Position lifecycle:** study enter/continue/replace/exit-to-SAFE/short and dynamic management. Research horizons 1–63 do NOT impose fixed holding periods.
8. **Capital competition:** compare simultaneous opportunities, combination complementarity, and SAFE/cash on an absolute economic basis.
9. **Portfolio risk:** sizing, concentration, correlation, catastrophic loss and state-dependent exposure. Preserve the evidence chain to candidate and environment.
10. **Integration and validation:** assemble and test executable conditional policies; report portfolio CAGR, MDD, tail risk, turnover, costs, robustness and failure episodes. These outcomes must not retroactively rewrite exploratory evidence.

## Research evidence model
Each evaluated candidate/combination must have reproducible identifiers, chromosome definitions, composition (1–4), causal context, entry timestamp, applicable universe and fold, daily horizons 1–63, EV and MAE (with clear sign/unit definitions), costs, sample and independent-episode counts, uncertainty, constraints, and provenance. Preserve candidates and negative findings; do not collapse to a small global-winner shortlist. Rank/filter by conditional environment, EV, MAE, horizon, and later portfolio metrics without destroying the underlying catalog. Record interactions, not merely standalone chromosome performance.
