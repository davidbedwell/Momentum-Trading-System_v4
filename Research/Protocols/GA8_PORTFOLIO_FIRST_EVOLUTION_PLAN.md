# GA8 — Portfolio-First Opportunity-Directed Evolution (controlling plan)

Status: approved design and engineering handoff, October 2026. New GA8, not GA6/GA7 renamed. Preserve GA6/GA7 archives and baseline reproducibility. Implement in isolated GA8 source under MTS v4 with runtime workspace /opt/mts/ga8.

## Mission and nonnegotiable targets
Discover profitable, niche-specialized entry, management and exit behaviors and complementary gene-family combinations on the real DEV79 2022–2026 universe. Evaluate a single shared funded capital portfolio, not a sum of standalone chromosome metrics. Hard requirement funded PORTFOLIO maximum drawdown <20%; funded PORTFOLIO Calmar >1.5; maximize funded CAGR, aspirational >=35% annually. These are search targets, not promises. If none qualifies, return no survivor and report the best infeasible frontier without relabeling failure as success. Explicit NO_TRADE, cash and SGOV are valid choices. Never force allocation.

## Search architecture
1. Opportunity discovery: for each stock/session compute only time-t-available features (momentum, trend, volatility, volume, breadth, relative strength, market regime, niche distance). Compute forward outcomes solely as retrospective training labels; enforce chronological isolation. Search across 8 gene families: trend, momentum, volatility, volume, recursive, sector, breadth, relative strength; allow AND/OR/grouped combinations, conditional activation, niche specialization and independent entry/hold/add/reduce/exit behaviors. Disable sector features until dated point-in-time sector data exists; sector label absence must not stop price-only engine. No leakage through fitted thresholds, membership, normalization, or forward labels.
2. Niche eligibility: compute causal controlling niche each day from trailing features and frozen or expanding historical calibration, choose closest overlapping niche; search specialist chromosomes first, then related niches and proven transferable candidates. Preserve niche diversity and shadow nonselected eligible candidates; abstain if thresholds unmet. GA4 entries without reliable exits are research-only, not tradable.
3. Portfolio manager: shared initial $100,000, next-open fills, settled cash, fees/slippage, corporate actions, earnings exclusion if reliable timestamped earnings data is available, crash A/B/B1/C and R-1 only when input tape is causal. Explicit per-symbol cap 10% in learning (30% only separately validated), per-trade risk 1–2%, evaluate -1% and exit by -2% as user policy, 2–3 names per sector when reliable, correlation-aware diversification and total risk cap. Evolve allocation, scaling, exits, replacement, reserved cash and SGOV. Shadow outcomes independently replay full native accounting and are never substituted for funded equity.
4. Marginal portfolio contribution: evaluate candidate versus same manager/market path without candidate, accounting for reallocated capital, drawdown, turnover and fees; seek complementary exposures, not redundant correlated clones. Use cheap screen then faithful funded evaluation; fitness feasible-first: hard MDD <20%, Calmar >1.5, then maximize funded CAGR and robust risk-adjusted performance, with trade frequency, downside tail, concentration, stability and costs as guardrails. Pareto preserve specialists and novelty, adapt search, no global winner bias.
5. Evolution: initialize all families and combinations, sample structurally diverse parents, niche-aware mutation/crossover, survivor selection based on funded incremental benefit plus niche specialization; include an incumbent no-change challenger and independent novelty challengers. Stage 0 functionality smoke (2-3 complementary strategies, real trades), stage 1 small measured pilot, stage 2 moderate generations, stage 3 full exploration only after evidence of learning. Record generation-by-generation portfolio metrics, families, niche coverage, trade ledger, selection decisions, hyperparameters, seeds and resource usage. Stop on no improvement with 100-generation stagnation over a multiobjective frontier, time/resource budget, or invalid data. No long run without ETA and stop conditions.
6. Chronological separation: develop on early 2022–2026 sessions, evaluate walk-forward later segments; preserve untouched final validation window. Do not optimize against final validation. All family comparisons use same execution assumptions, time windows, cash/SGOV and defense; benchmark SPY and cash when available. DEV79 fixed cohort is research, not point-in-time index membership proof. Include survivorship/PIT limitations.

## Delivery sequence and acceptance gates
A. Inventory exact existing data and grammar; 79 symbols and 92,502 bars available 2022-01-03 through 2026-09-14. Sector history is static, so disable sector-dependent genes/limits pending dated data; use price-only correlation diversification. Identify other missing causal inputs explicitly. Do not stop entire engine for optional features.
B. Build standalone GA8 package, configs, data adapter, causal feature/niche engine, native entry/exit grammar, funded next-open simulator, independent shadow ledger, risk controller and reproducible tests. No future leakage or cash creation. Compare NAV to cash plus marked holdings, and bound MDD 0–100% for nonnegative NAV. Benchmark baseline.
C. Execute real-data smoke with 2–3 deliberately distinct strategy families, demonstrate entries, holds, exits, scaling, capital competition, abstention, risk and P&L attribution; show funded CAGR/MDD/Calmar, fills, costs, drawdown path. Fix functionality before extended certification.
D. Implement GA breeding and marginal-contribution selection, run short bounded generations with multiple families; prove offspring and manager updates alter actual funded decisions, and provide parent/offspring lineage and diversity ledger. Track best feasible and infeasible portfolios and per-niche specialist coverage.
E. Run sequential chronological folds with no future-tuned parameters, then one untouched validation; report best portfolios and gene-family combinations with honest trade counts, CAGR, MDD, Calmar, exposure, cost, turnover, benchmark and fold variation. Never claim edge on in-sample metrics alone.
F. Package scripts, tests, requirements, runbook, reproducible configuration, reports and machine-readable metrics. Git commit to MTS v4 branch and push only when permitted, with exact commit hash. Do not overwrite GA6/GA7.

## Supervision contract
Codex executes stages autonomously and writes status.json with stage, completed checks, current command/PID, elapsed time, throughput, ETA, blockers and next action. At stage boundaries give measured ETA before scaling; keep 8 workers or available capacity productive without excessive concurrency. Stop and report if required price data or execution parity truly blocks real-data pilot. Avoid endless preflight/certification or synthetic substitutes. User can inspect status anytime. Engineering goal is functioning end-to-end GA8, then evidence of an edge.
