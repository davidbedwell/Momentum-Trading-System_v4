## Controlling scientific architecture
1. Point-in-time causality is foundational: at historical decision date T, every predictive input and contextual relationship must be constructible solely from information available at or before T.
2. Soft hierarchy: MARKET STATE -> CONTEXTUAL PEER/GROUP STATE -> STOCK STATE -> CONDITIONAL STRATEGY/APPLICABILITY -> ABSOLUTE OPPORTUNITY VS SAFE -> CAPITAL ALLOCATION/LIFECYCLE.
3. Contextual peer/group layer is improved from rigid sector-only design:
   - PIT formal sector/industry classification may be used when historically valid evidence exists.
   - PIT dynamic peer relationships are first-class and are derived causally from trailing information ending at T.
   - Current/future GICS classifications MUST NOT be projected backward as predictive historical labels.
   - Group labels/cluster IDs MUST NOT become ticker-identity proxies.
   - GA may use formal sector context, dynamic peer context, both, or neither.
4. Market, group, stock, and strategy layers are soft information levels, not hard gates.
5. Strategy modules have state-conditioned applicability and may mix primitives across strategy families.
6. Opportunity is absolute/economic, not merely cross-sectional rank. Market/R1 inputs must have non-cancelled paths to opportunity, risk appetite, SAFE hurdle, and/or exposure.
7. SAFE is a genuine capital competitor.
8. Horizon-free lifecycle: ENTER, CONTINUE, REPLACE, EXIT_TO_SAFE, SHORT emerge from current opportunity/friction/uncertainty competition. No fixed holding duration, cooldown, position age, or entry-anchored decision state.
9. Exactly four allocation modes: equal, strength, uncertainty, downside.
10. No position caps, sector caps, leverage, or options. Gross exposure <= 1.
11. Primary evolutionary objectives remain: maximize CAGR; minimize absolute MDD. Claude-proposed numeric guard thresholds are NOT approved and MUST NOT silently enter fitness.
12. Economic realism and diagnostics follow the accepted Claude recommendations where they do not introduce an unapproved numeric scientific threshold.
