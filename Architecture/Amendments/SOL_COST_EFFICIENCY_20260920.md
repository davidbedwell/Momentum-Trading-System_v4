# Sol Cost-Efficiency Amendment — 2026-09-20

## Authority
Human-approved engineering response to the UBER Direct-Sol telemetry audit. The purpose is to reduce paid Sol calls and transported tokens without weakening MTS scientific governance or transferring scientific authority from the AI Research Director to deterministic Analysis.

## Frozen boundary
Analysis prepares the laboratory; Sol decides what experiment to conduct.

Deterministic code may precompute and transport neutral, reusable arithmetic and source organization when that work is scientifically nonselective. It must not choose hypotheses, rank relationships, select thresholds, decide significance, suppress evidence, prioritize research directions, or filter the discovery space presented to Sol.

## Approved changes
1. Compact BEGIN transport by omitting bulky precomputed relationship/calendar bodies while preserving catalogs, schemas, coverage, reusable dataset access, and Sol access to underlying evidence.
2. Precompute neutral FINRA weekly source-defined categories, counts, and share quantities.
3. Precompute catalyst/earnings inventory and represented release-clock classification without assigning materiality, causation, direction, or predictive significance.
4. Extend point-in-time SEC share structure with volume/shares-outstanding turnover, a human-authorized prior-20-session turnover mean that excludes the current session, and current/prior-20 turnover ratio. No threshold or signal is created.
5. Provide a robust reusable rolling numeric Analysis transform so RD-selected rolling mean/sum/min/max/slope work can execute in one lineage-preserving pass instead of fragile compose/index chains. Window, lag, field, statistic, and scientific use remain RD-selected.

## Explicit non-changes
- Sol remains the scientific Research Director.
- No lower-cost model is inserted between evidence and Sol.
- No UBER-discovered hypothesis is made a mandatory agenda for virgin tickers.
- No deterministic ranking or relationship selection is introduced.
- No validation, candidacy, expectancy, or promotion threshold is weakened.
- Raw evidence and reusable derived datasets remain available to Sol.

## Measurement target
Use the next governed virgin-ticker execution to measure actual Sol calls, input/output tokens, and spend against the UBER Direct-Sol reference. The engineering target is approximately $4/ticker from the recent $5–6/ticker expectation, but scientific completeness and unchanged authority take precedence over the cost target.
