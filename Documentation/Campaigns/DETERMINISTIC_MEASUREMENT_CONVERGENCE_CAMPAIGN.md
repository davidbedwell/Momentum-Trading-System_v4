# Momentum Trading System — Deterministic Measurement Convergence Campaign

## Purpose

Establish the first evidence-driven routine Intake measurement set without prematurely removing deterministic tools that may prove useful to Analysis.

This is a calibration campaign and implementation milestone, not a permanent architectural limit.

## Campaign Model

The shared governed Deterministic Computation Library is available to both Data Intake and Analysis.

During the calibration campaign, Data Intake materializes the complete applicable governed deterministic measurement set for each selected ticker. Analysis may use those measurements and may also invoke any other governed deterministic computation required by a Research Proposal.

Analysis performs the analytical work needed to characterize historically relevant outcome regions, including proximal opportunity regions, comparable failures, liquidity-trap regions, and appropriate controls. Research Director directs subsequent research through Research Nexus.

## Five-Ticker Cohorts

The campaign proceeds in cohorts of five completed tickers.

After every five-ticker cohort, Research Director shall initiate a **Measurement Convergence Assessment** before the next cohort is treated as additional calibration evidence.

The assessment shall:

1. Rerank deterministic measurements using all accumulated evidence.
2. Reassess measurement classifications, including:
   - core contributors;
   - conditional contributors;
   - interaction-only contributors;
   - redundant measurements;
   - false-signal-prone measurements;
   - unstable measurements;
   - low-contribution measurements; and
   - measurements with insufficient evidence.
3. Ask Analysis retrospectively whether any completed analysis would have materially benefited from measurements or computational tools outside the currently favored set.
4. Identify recurring analytical deficiencies, missing measurements, missing transformations, or missing computational capabilities.
5. Compare the current ranking and classifications with those produced after the preceding cohort.
6. Preserve the ranking/classification history rather than overwriting prior cohort results.
7. Determine whether sufficient convergence has been reached.

## Convergence Criterion

**Sufficient convergence exists when successive ticker cohorts produce materially stable measurement rankings/classifications and retrospective tool-coverage review identifies no important recurring analytical deficiency.**

No fixed ticker count determines completion.

If sufficient convergence has not been reached, another five-ticker cohort is processed and the assessment repeats.

## Post-Convergence Action

Once sufficient convergence exists:

- Research Director produces the evidence-based measurement ranking and convergence report.
- The routinely materialized Intake measurement set may be revised using that evidence.
- Usefulness ranking and computational/materialization cost remain distinct considerations.
- Lower-ranked measurements are not deleted from the Deterministic Computation Library.
- Analysis retains access to the complete governed library for future Research Proposals.
- Future evidence may trigger reranking and revision of the routine Intake set.

## Scientific Safeguard

A measurement's lack of use is not, by itself, evidence of lack of value. A measurement may be downgraded for non-contribution only when the research process provided a reasonable opportunity for its contribution to be evaluated.

The campaign is intended to discover convergence, not force it.
