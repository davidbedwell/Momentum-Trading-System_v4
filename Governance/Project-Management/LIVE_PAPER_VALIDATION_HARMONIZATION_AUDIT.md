# Live Paper Validation Harmonization Audit

## Scope

This audit records integration of Live Paper Validation into existing canonical
MTS architecture and standards.

## Canonical Progression

Broad or sufficiently representative Decision evaluation precedes campaign-level
evaluation of successful and failed outcomes.

MTS then completes prospective Live Paper Validation using the initial $10,000
finite simulated portfolio under live market conditions.

Passing paper validation establishes eligibility for a separate
live-authorization process and never automatically grants live brokerage
authority.

## Authority Preservation

Live Paper Validation is not an additional research gate and Accountability
does not approve each paper trade.

Decision retains investment judgment within governance. Execution implements
authorized Decisions. Learning evaluates campaign evidence. Live brokerage
authority remains separately granted.

## Portfolio Integrity

Paper validation is stateful. Capital committed to positions, realized and
unrealized results, concentration, reserved capital, and buying power affect
subsequent Decisions.
